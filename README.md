## Tyran

[Play Tyran](fun/tyran/index.html) is a ten-sector scrolling top-down shooter with solo and two-player flight. Each sector has a dedicated terrain palette and destructible scenery, plus a ship palette reserved for enemies so hostile craft stay readable against the ground.

- The terrain is built as five parallax planes: a slow underground or water substrate, painted ground, rocks and shrubs, trees and tall vegetation, then clouds, high trees and structures.
- Enemy rounds scale with hull class: small ships fire compact, low-damage spectrum rounds while heavier craft use larger, brighter shapes. A cap keeps hostile fire from overwhelming the frame rate.
- Every fighter keeps a fixed nose direction and swaps between three cached side-tilt sprites as it banks, so silhouettes stay crisp without per-frame rotation.
- Six weapon profiles, formations, boss weak points and timed armor windows give every upgrade path a useful role.
- The interface uses sentence-case copy and an Oxanium display face for the flight-command feel; Space Grotesk remains the readable body face.
