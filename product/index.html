Products

Eight instruments.

Seven plug-ins for VST3, Audio Unit and Standalone hosts, and one Rack Extension for the Reason rack. Every note is synthesised as you play it: none of them replays a recording.

01 / Rack Extension

YouKnow

A circuit-modelled analogue polysynth for Reason 14 and later: a direct subtractive signal path, stereo chorus, 77 original patches, and engine quality you can scale to the session.

Product page →
02 / DCO polysynth

YouKnow106

A six-voice DCO polysynth modelled block by block on the 1984 Roland Juno-106, shipping all 128 original factory tone-memory states in the hardware's own order.

Product page →
03 / Drum instrument

Drumalor

Thirteen drum voices, every hit generated as you play it from oscillators, noise, filters and resonators. No samples and no ROM images.

Product page →
04 / Modelled guitar

Electry

An eight-string Drop-E electric guitar modelled string by string, with a fretting hand that decides where in the neck a phrase actually lands.

Product page →
05 / Modelled guitar

Acustra

An acoustic guitar synthesised from six string models, a passive measured bridge and a measurement-derived body, behind ten panel controls.

Product page →
06 / Modelled taiko

Taikor

Four taiko drums and four strokes on a 4 by 4 keyboard grid, every stroke solved from a struck circular membrane rather than replayed.

Product page →
07 / Virtual analog

Septum

A ten-voice virtual-analog synthesizer modelling the Roland SH-201, with two complete tones in every patch and a seven-saw SUPER SAW.

Product page →
08 / Monophonic synth

Ghostar

A monophonic synthesizer modelled from the documentation of the 1983 Crumar Spirit, built around the series dual filter that gives it its voice.
