The accordion

Brass reeds and moving wind, with no samples.

Pressure changes bend pitch slightly and wake upper harmonics naturally; chords pull more air from the bellows, and Grille closes or opens the tone cover.

  • Auto or Mod-wheel bellows

    Let automatic bellows push cleanly behind a keyboard part, or shape every attack, fade and direction change on the modulation wheel.

  • Eight registers & Musette

    Switch Bassoon, Bandoneon, Accordion, Organ, Violin, Master, Clarinet and Piccolo, with adjustable Musette detuning on twin treble reeds.

  • Left-hand bass & chords

    Trigger deep fundamentals and fixed Stradella chord voicings on the lower manual beside treble playing on one keyboard.

  • Four accordion types in 0.8.0

    Upcoming 0.8.0 builds add Type selection—Default, Irish (C#/D dry box), German (bright Styrian) and Balkan—expanding the bank from 9 to 15 patches.

In the rack

Stereo output and Expression CV.

Patch CV into the back panel to modulate bellows air outside the mod wheel. Release candidate 0.8.0 moves Room up to the front setup bar and leaves clean stereo routing and Expression CV on the rear.

Reason Rack Extension

Pick a register and play.

Start on Master or Musette with automatic bellows, then switch Bellows to Manual and sweep the mod wheel to breathe each chord like a real box.

Rack Extension version history

Respira release history.

Version 0.1.27 is live in the Reason Shop. Build 0.10.0f1 tunes each accordion type as its school does and removes the phaser-like near-field colour.

0.10.0f1 Release candidate · 27 November 2026 · built

The four accordion types differ more in their physical make-up, and the near-field microphone no longer adds a phaser-like colour.

0.9.0f1 Release candidate · 25 November 2026 · built

Each type is tuned as its school tunes it, and the Irish box presses and draws.

0.8.0f3 Release candidate · 23 November 2026

Oversampling and Eco move to the back and keep editable front readouts, so both map with Remote and neither is automated.

0.8.0f2 Release candidate source · 21 November 2026 · source only

Gives every property human-readable Remote names and puts Type (Default, Irish, German, Balkan) and Room onto the front setup bar so everything maps in Reason Remote.

0.8.0f1 Release candidate · 21 November 2026 · built

Brings canonical Respira 0.8.0: four distinct accordion types with size-matched acoustics, 15 factory patches, and +60% bellows air and reflections.

0.7.2f1 Release candidate · 20 November 2026 · built

Added the dark red velvet and mother-of-pearl cabinet with interactive register tiles and bass/chord lamps.

0.1.27 Reason Shop release · 18 November 2026 · device 0.1.27f1

Public Reason Shop launch with eight registers, Stradella bass, auto/manual bellows and nine factory patches (with 0.1.27f4 adding full Remote and Mute/Solo compliance).
