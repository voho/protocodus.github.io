Respira

Respira is an accordion in which no reed sounds unless air moves. With Hand on Auto, the default, the bellows play themselves while you hold keys, in time with Reason’s transport, and the wheel swells soft notes and eases stronger notes back. Switch to Wheel and the mod wheel is your hand: rock it back and forth to pull and push air through the reeds, and every phrase swells and turns with your strokes. The Rack edition is in development; final specifications will be confirmed at release.

The accordion

Air in, sound out.

Opening the bellows draws air through the pull reeds; closing them presses it through the push reeds. How fast you move sets the pressure, and the pressure sets how strongly the reeds speak.

  • The bellows is the instrument

    Auto draws a person’s strokes, never quite even, with a planned turn at each change of direction. On Wheel, Bellows sets how much air one sweep moves. Assist eases the extra air a full chord needs, and Air opens the valve so you can reposition the bellows in silence. The gauge swings between pull and push as you play.

  • Every reed is a steel tongue

    Each sounding reed is a steel tongue in its own chamber, set vibrating by the air flowing past it. Velocity sets how quickly a key opens its reeds, and pitch shifts slightly with pressure, as on a real reed. Aftertouch lets a held key rise part way, so the note softens and bends with the bellows.

  • Eight registers

    Clarinet, Bassoon, Violin, Musette, Bandoneon, Organ, Oboe and Master combine five ranks of reeds. Musette sets how far the tremolo reeds are detuned, from unison to a wide, beating tremolo.

  • Bass and chord buttons

    The lowest octave plays the bass buttons, the next the chord buttons, and everything from C3 up plays the treble. Keyboard, on the back, switches to Full to play the whole range as treble. The bass and chord reeds sound through the bass box, the space under the bass end. An accordion has no pitch bend, and neither does Respira.

  • The mechanism

    Keys knock as they land on their felts and pallets as their springs seat them, and the bass and chord buttons, register switches and air button knock too, with or without air. Mechanics, on the back, sets how loud they are.

In the rack

Your hand on the left. Registers on the grille.

The four-unit front panel puts the hand on the bellows cloth: the mod wheel between an open and a closed accordion, the Wheel and Auto tabs and the pressure gauge. The nickel grille carries the eight register tabs, with Bellows, Assist, Musette, Grille, Width, Bass and Volume below. The sustain pedal opens the air valve too.

Turn the rack around for Keyboard, Momentum, which sets how smoothly the bellows turn at each change of direction, Mechanics for the level of the keys’ and bellows’ noises, Room, a chart of the registers, oversampling and the stereo output. Life starts at 50% and varies the reeds, bellows effort and microphone movement. Touch shapes Auto bellows emphasis from your playing. Init and every factory patch start at a subtle 0.5% Room level, with fine control near dry.

Reason Rack Extension

Start with a stroke.

Load a patch and hold a chord; Auto does the rest until you take the wheel. Eight factory patches, one for each register, sit at matching levels, with Init to build your own. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.

Respira development history.

The latest Rack Extension work in progress. Availability will be confirmed at release.

0.1.12d1 Rack Extension candidate · 3 November 2026

In Auto, keyboard velocity and the modulation wheel shape bellows speed. Bass and chord buttons now keep shared reeds sounding until the last button lifts, and Life controls the strength of the reed variations.

0.1.10d1 Development build · 3 November 2026

Refines reed airflow, chamber losses and the way reed ranks share their air supply. Bellows compliance, keybed resonances, opening chiff, player tremor, microphone shading, folds, straps and valves add detail through the existing controls.

Reason Shop publication is separate from this development build.
