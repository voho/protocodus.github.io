The accordion

Steel reeds and moving air, with no samples.

Bellows pressure sets how loud each reed plays, how quickly it speaks and how its tone and pitch move. A full chord asks for more air than a single note, velocity sets how fast a key opens, and aftertouch partly closes a held key for a softer, bending note.

  • Auto or Wheel bellows

    Auto works the bellows while you play and follows how hard you strike; C-1 pulls and C♯-1 pushes. Wheel puts them on the mod wheel, with Momentum for the weight of the arm and Assist for the extra air a chord needs.

  • Eight registers & Musette

    Clarinet, Bassoon, Violin, Musette, Bandoneon, Organ, Oboe and Master over five treble reed ranks. Musette sets the tremolo's beating, Grille the treble's brightness and Bass the left hand's level.

  • Bass and chord buttons

    Split plays bass and chord buttons below C2 and the treble from C2 to C7; Full plays the whole keyboard as treble.

  • Life & Mechanics

    Life adds a player's and an instrument's unevenness. Keys, pallets and switches knock, valves slap and the bellows folds rustle, at the level Mechanics sets.

  • Four accordion types (0.10.0 builds)

    Type chooses Default, Irish, German or Balkan, each with its own reeds, blocks, bellows, bass box and cabinet, plus six patches for them. Built, not yet in the Shop.

In the rack

Stereo output with Note and Gate CV.

The back holds Keyboard (Split or Full) with a map of every key, Note and Gate CV, Oversampling, Room and the main stereo pair. 0.10.0 builds add Eco and a setup bar on the front with Type, Keyboard, Room and the Oversampling readouts.

Reason Rack Extension

Pick a register and play.

Start on Init in Auto and play. Try the register tabs, then select Wheel and rock the mod wheel to work the bellows yourself.

Respira release history.

Version 0.1.27 is live in the Reason Shop. Build 0.10.0f1 tunes each accordion type as its school does and removes the phaser-like near-field colour.

The four accordion types differ more in their physical make-up, and the near-field microphone no longer adds a phaser-like colour.

0.9.0f1 Release candidate · 25 November 2026 · built

Each type is tuned as its school tunes it, and the Irish box presses and draws.

0.8.0f3 Release candidate · 23 November 2026

Oversampling and Eco move to the back and keep editable front readouts, so both map with Remote and neither is automated.

0.8.0f2 Release candidate source · 21 November 2026 · source only

Gives every property human-readable Remote names and puts Type (Default, Irish, German, Balkan) and Room onto the front setup bar so everything maps in Reason Remote.

0.8.0f1 Release candidate · 21 November 2026 · built

Brings canonical Respira 0.8.0: four distinct accordion types with size-matched acoustics, 15 factory patches, and +60% bellows air and reflections.

Public Reason Shop launch with eight registers, Stradella bass, auto/manual bellows and nine factory patches (with 0.1.27f4 adding full Remote and Mute/Solo compliance).
