Respira

Respira is an accordion you play with the bellows. The mod wheel is your hand: rock it back and forth to pull and push air through the reeds, and every phrase swells, breathes and turns with your strokes. Hold the wheel still and the sound dies away, because nothing sounds unless air moves. The Rack edition is in development; final specifications will be confirmed at release.

The accordion

Air in, sound out.

Opening the bellows draws air through the pull reeds; closing them presses it through the push reeds. How fast you move sets the pressure, and the pressure sets how strongly the reeds speak.

  • The bellows is the instrument

    Bellows sets how much air one sweep of the wheel moves. Assist eases the extra air a full chord needs, and Air opens the valve so you can reposition the bellows in silence. The gauge swings between pull and push as you play.

  • Every reed is a steel tongue

    Each sounding reed is a steel tongue in its own chamber, set vibrating by the air flowing past it. Velocity sets how quickly a key opens its reeds, and pitch shifts slightly with pressure, as on a real reed.

  • Eight registers

    Clarinet, Bassoon, Violin, Musette, Bandoneon, Organ, Oboe and Master combine five ranks of reeds. Musette sets how far the tremolo reeds are detuned, from unison to a wide, beating tremolo.

  • Bass and chord buttons

    The lowest octave plays the bass buttons, the next the chord buttons, and everything from C3 up plays the treble. Switch Keyboard to Full to play the whole range as treble. The pitch wheel bends every sounding reed.

In the rack

Your hand on the left. Registers on the grille.

The four-unit front panel puts the hand on the bellows cloth: the pitch and hand wheels, the Air button, the keyboard split and the pressure gauge. The nickel grille carries the eight register tabs, with Bellows, Assist, Musette, Grille, Width, Bass and Volume below. The sustain pedal opens the air valve too.

Turn the rack around for Momentum, which sets how smoothly the bellows turn at each change of direction, a chart of the registers and the dry stereo output, ready for your own room and effects.

Reason Rack Extension

Start with a stroke.

Load a patch, hold a chord and rock the mod wheel. Eight factory patches, one for each register, sit at matching levels, with Init to build your own. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.
