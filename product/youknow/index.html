Circuit-modelled analogue polysynth

YouKnow

A six-voice circuit-modelled analogue polysynth with a direct subtractive signal path, a stereo bucket-brigade chorus, and calibrated circuit tolerances you can dial from nominal to exaggerated. It comes in two editions: a Rack Extension for Reason, and a plug-in for VST3, Audio Unit and Standalone hosts.

Inside the instrument

Familiar panel,
modelled block by block.

The panel follows the signal: LFO and DCO through HPF, VCF, VCA, ENV and CHORUS, with performance and keyboard controls below. Both editions run the same voice architecture.

01

A direct subtractive path

LFO sets modulation rate and delayed onset. DCO selects octave range, waveforms, PWM source and depth, sub level, noise and oscillator modulation. HPF gives four high-pass positions including bass boost, and VCF adds cutoff, resonance, envelope polarity and amount, LFO amount and keyboard tracking.

02

Four chorus states

Two uncompanded 256-stage MN3009 delay lines driven anti-phase by one triangle, at rates derived from the instrument's own oscillator circuit. Off, I, II and a narrow I+II combination are all available, with a Chorus Hiss control beside them.

03

A filter that self-oscillates

The four-pole filter is solved directly as its continuous nonlinear equations, with cutoff anchored on Roland's own service calibration point where converter code 6272 self-oscillates at 248 Hz, which the model predicts within a cent rather than fitting.

04

Unit Character and Aging

Unit Character scales every modelled tolerance from calibrated-nominal at 0 percent, through the 100 percent default described as matching real hardware, to exaggerated at 200 percent. Aging sits beside it and drifts each voice's filter trim flat by its own share of up to a quarter tone while lifting the noise source by up to 3.5 dB.

Troubleshooting

If something is wrong.

If notes arrive but there is no sound, load Init or reset the device, verify Volume and VCA Level, and confirm both stereo outputs are connected. For high CPU on the Rack Extension, select 1x, Poly, Cubic and Normal on the rear and reduce Voices. If a quality change seems delayed, release all notes and let the output tail settle; the change is applied only at a safe silent boundary. For no CV response, connect both Gate and Note CV.

What it does not do

Stated plainly.

Neither edition models pitch drift or inter-voice detune, so six voices on one key are always exactly in tune, and unison carries no detune generator. Mechanisms whose magnitude the sources cannot fix are deliberately left out, including chorus wet-mute click and leakage, high-pass mode-change transients, converter charge injection, and the common output amplifier's untrimmed even-order distortion. The Rack Extension does not expose MIDI Program Change or SysEx file import and export; use Reason's patch browser and patch files instead, and note that its rear Note and Gate CV pair is monophonic. Unit Character, Aging, Chorus Hiss and the four rear engine policies are available to Remote but are deliberately left outside sequencer automation.
