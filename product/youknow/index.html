The sound

The character is in the circuitry.

From the attack of a bass note to the movement inside a held chord, the model follows the stages that make the sound and the way they interact.

  • Voices with a history

    Mix saw, pulse, sub and noise. Oscillator and sub-divider states carry on between notes, while modelled component differences give the voice cards their own character.

  • A filter you can play

    Shape the tone with a nonlinear four-pole resonant low-pass filter, a four-position high-pass filter and a shared ADSR envelope. Push resonance into self-oscillation or let the envelope open a bright attack.

  • The chorus is part of the instrument

    Two clocked BBD delay lines add the movement of Chorus I and II. Modelled signal holding, transfer loss, saturation and surrounding filters shape the texture. Try the narrow I+II combination and set the hiss with Chorus Noise.

Playing

From a focused lead to a full chord.

The familiar signal path stays immediate, with room to shape the response to your playing.

  • Poly 1, Poly 2 and Unison

    Choose the voice assignment that fits the part, add portamento and set up to 16 voices. Version 1.3 refines Unison glide and uses smooth level transitions when changing modes.

  • 99 sounds plus Init

    The 100-patch bank includes basses, leads, pads, strings and more, with an Init patch for starting from scratch. Browse, save and recall sounds through Reason’s native patch controls.

  • Character and Aging

    Unit Character adjusts circuit variation and saves with the patch. Aging explores service drift and noise changes and stays with the song, so browsing sounds preserves your chosen state.

  • Modulation with phrasing

    Use the LFO for pitch, filter and pulse-width movement. Rear Sync LFO lets Rate and delayed modulation onset follow song-tempo note lengths.

  • Expression in the performance

    Velocity response, pitch and modulation wheels, sustain and glide connect the controls to the part you play. Sound and performance settings are available to Reason automation and Remote.

  • Choose your processing detail

    Rear controls offer 1×, 2× or 4× oversampling, saturation calculations, interpolation and filter solvers. Front status displays show the current choices. The efficient default is 1×, Poly, Cubic and Normal.

In the rack

Connect the circuit to your track.

Eight rear CV inputs cover Note, Gate, Cutoff, Resonance, Volume, Amp, Sub and Noise. Use Note and Gate for a monophonic CV part, or play polyphonically from MIDI.

Stereo audio outputs connect to your mixer and effects. All 42 custom sound, performance and quality controls support sequencer automation and Remote mapping, alongside Reason’s standard wheels and sustain.
