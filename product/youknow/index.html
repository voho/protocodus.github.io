Inside the instrument

The sound comes from the circuit.

Every voice runs the full signal chain—clock-driven oscillator, nonlinear filter and VCA, high-pass network and stereo BBD chorus—without sampled waveforms.

  • Clocked DCO, sub & noise

    Saw and pulse waves in 16′, 8′ and 4′ ranges beside a square sub-oscillator and noise, with pulse width moved by the LFO or set by hand.

  • HPF & self-oscillating VCF

    A four-position high-pass filter, with a bass boost at 0, into a resonant low-pass filter with envelope polarity, LFO and keyboard tracking that self-oscillates at high resonance.

  • Chorus I, II and I+II

    Bucket-brigade chorus whose sweep and hiss follow measurements of an original chorus board, with the hiss on its own slider.

  • Voices, Unit Character & Aging

    Six voices by default and up to 16, in Poly 1, Poly 2 or Unison, with glide and velocity. Unit Character varies component tolerances and saves with the patch; Aging adds service drift and noise.

In the rack

Eight CV inputs and the unit model on the back.

Every section has its own row of sliders on the 8U front. The back holds Note, Gate, Cutoff, Resonance, Volume, Amp, Sub and Noise CV, the Left/Mono and Right outputs, Quality with 1x, 2x or 4x oversampling and Normal or Max circuit rate, Sync LFO, and Unit Character and Aging.

Rack & open-source native edition

Built for Reason, backed by an open-source DSP core.

The Rack Extension ships with 128 factory patches: 127 sounds from basses, brass and plucks to pads, strings, leads and effects, plus Init. Its C++ circuit engine is also developed openly as the open-source VST3, AU and CLAP edition (currently v1.4.11; see known gaps and native changelog).

Rack Extension version history

YouKnow release history.

Version 1.4.11 is live in the Reason Shop. Build 1.4.11f5 on Rack main makes the eight front readouts editable, so each can be mapped with Remote.

1.4.11 Reason Shop release · device 1.4.11f4

A new classic hardware panel, a chorus that follows an original board’s measured sweep, hiss and level, a stronger sub-oscillator, every patch re-levelled with 28 new ones, and sequencer Mute and Solo that silence the device.

1.4.11f5 Release candidate · 21 November 2026 · built

All eight front-panel readouts (Quality, three VCF modes, Circuit Rate, Calibration, Aging and LFO Sync) become editable value displays with Remote mapping support.

1.4.11f4 Release candidate · 18 November 2026 · built

Updates the Create menu and browser name to YouKnow Polysynth, completes 128 factory patches, and follows 1.4.11f3’s full Remote mapping, routing symbols and key-following Note On lamp.

1.4.5f1 – 1.4.10f3 Development updates · 3–14 November 2026

Added VCF models, Calibration and Aging trims, LFO key sync, Mute and Solo routing, 8-voice unison chorus refinements, and expanded the Rack factory bank to 128 presets.

1.3.5 Current Reason Shop release · 5 October 2026

Added 100 factory patches, Component Variance control, chorus gain alignment, and earlier 1.2.0–1.3.0 hardware model improvements.
