Features

What it does.

  • Six modelled voices, playable up to sixteen
  • Direct subtractive path: LFO, DCO, HPF, VCF, VCA and envelope
  • Four-pole filter that self-oscillates at full resonance
  • Stereo bucket-brigade chorus: off, I, II, and a narrow I+II
  • Unit Character, from calibrated-nominal to exaggerated
  • Aging, for the drift a serviced instrument picks up
  • Engine quality you scale against CPU, 1x to 4x
  • Monophonic Note and Gate CV in the Reason rack
  • 77 original patches, or the 128 classic factory tones
  • Offline audio processing, no telemetry, no network

Reason 14 or later · macOS 11 or later · VST3, Audio Unit, Standalone
