Inside the device

Familiar panel,
scalable engine.

The front panel follows the signal: LFO and DCO through HPF, VCF, VCA, ENV and CHORUS, with performance and keyboard controls in the lower bays. The engine policies that cost CPU live on the rear, out of the way of the sequencer.

01

A direct subtractive path

LFO sets modulation rate and delayed onset. DCO selects octave range, waveforms, PWM source and depth, sub level, noise and oscillator modulation. HPF gives four high-pass positions including bass boost, and VCF adds cutoff, resonance, envelope polarity and amount, LFO amount and keyboard tracking. At high resonance the filter can self-oscillate.

02

Four chorus states

Off, I, II, and a dedicated narrow I+II combination, with Chorus Hiss beside them in the same bay. Init and patches that inherit the engine default use the quieter MN3009 typical signal-to-noise estimate; Chorus Hiss at 100 percent retains the datasheet maximum.

03

Engine quality you choose

Quality (1x, 2x, 4x), VCF Tanh (Exact, Fast, Poly), Fast Early (Hermite, Cubic) and VCF Solver (Max, High, Normal) are song-persistent rear-panel policies. New devices use the efficient defaults; a quality change waits for voices and tails to fall silent, then crosses through a short safety fade.

04

Unit Character and Aging

Unit Character varies calibrated circuit tolerances across 0 to 200 percent and is saved with the patch. Aging is a separate 0 to 100 percent control stored with the song, starting at 0 percent for a freshly serviced instrument; raising it moves per-voice filter trims by their share of up to a quarter tone and lifts the noise source by up to 3.5 dB.

Troubleshooting

If something is wrong.

If notes arrive but there is no sound, load Init or reset the device, verify Volume and VCA Level, and confirm both stereo outputs are connected. For high CPU, select 1x, Poly, Cubic and Normal on the rear and reduce Voices. If a quality change seems delayed, release all notes and let the output tail settle; the change is applied only at a safe silent boundary. For no CV response, connect both Gate and Note CV.

What it does not do

Stated plainly.

YouKnow does not expose MIDI Program Change or SysEx file import and export; use Reason's patch browser and patch files instead. The rear Note and Gate CV pair is monophonic, so CV plays one note at a time while MIDI stays polyphonic up to the selected Voices limit. Unit Character, Aging, Chorus Hiss and the four rear engine policies are available to Remote but are deliberately left outside sequencer automation. The Universal 45 development archive is for Reason Studios' build service and is not a customer installer.
