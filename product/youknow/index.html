Circuit-modelled polysynth

YouKnow

Pads, bass, strings and leads from a synth inspired by the Juno-106. Shape the oscillators, filter and stereo chorus on one panel, with adjustable polyphony and voice character.

  • 1–16 voices
  • Stereo chorus
  • 144 plug-in presets

For macOS, Windows and Linux. VST3 and standalone everywhere, with Audio Unit and CLAP on macOS and Windows.

The instrument

A familiar panel. Space to explore.

Build a sound from the oscillators up, or start with a preset and make it your own.

  • Oscillators and noise

    Combine saw and pulse waves, add a sub oscillator and blend in noise. Use pulse-width modulation to add movement to held notes.

  • Filter and envelope

    Shape swells, stabs and sweeps with a resonant low-pass filter and ADSR envelope. At full resonance, the filter can become a playable tone.

  • Stereo chorus

    Choose chorus I, II or I+II for different rates and textures. Set the chorus hiss separately from the effect.

  • Voice character

    Unit Character varies the modelled components between voices. Aging adds filter drift and noise.

  • Polyphony and unison

    Choose from one to sixteen voices for chords or stack voices on a single note. Add portamento to glide between notes.

  • Presets and SysEx

    The plug-in includes 144 presets plus INIT, with patch navigation and hardware-format SysEx import and export.

Reason Rack Extension

YouKnow, in the rack.

A dedicated panel for Reason, with its patch browser and a separate bank of 100 original Protocodus patches, including Init.

The Rack Extension has its own layout and release cycle. It requires Reason 14 or later and is currently in development.

In your setup

From a first patch to a saved session.

Start with a preset or INIT, then hover a control for help. Save your DAW project or a host preset to keep the setup. Quality settings let you balance oversampling against CPU use.
