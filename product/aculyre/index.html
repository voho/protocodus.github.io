The instruments

Open strings, one bridge bar and sympathetic shimmer.

Each string is a stiff vibrating string ending on one bridge bar over a hollow soundbox, and steel, nylon and gut lose their energy in their own ways, so the three instruments ring and fade differently.

  • Three instruments

    A 27-string lyre harp strung in steel or nylon and a 16-string gut lyre; the panel draws the chosen instrument and lights each string as it sounds, rings on or answers in sympathy.

  • Finger, nail or pick

    Position and Angle set where and in which direction you pluck; a harder pluck is brighter as well as louder, and a string that is still ringing grows its new note out of the old one.

  • Levers, sympathy and every key

    With Levers on Auto a black key flips the sharping lever on the string below, Sympathy sets how freely the other strings ring along, and keys past the strings retune the outermost one so every key of an 88-key keyboard sounds.

  • Microphones and piezo

    A stereo microphone pair with Room, or Piezo blending in the pickup under the bridge bar, all the way to the pickup alone. Release, Mute, the mod wheel and the sustain pedal shape the ringing.

In the rack

Width, piezo polarity and Note/Gate CV.

The rear panel holds Note and Gate CV, Oversampling (1x, 2x or 4x), Piezo polarity, Width and the stereo output, with a guide to what the wheels, the sustain pedal and the black keys do.

Reason Rack Extension

Nine patches across steel, nylon and gut.

Open Lyre Harp, Bright Nail Harp, Soft Nylon Close, Gut Lyre or Piezo Stage Harp, then switch the plucking hand or move Position along the string.

Rack Extension version history

Aculyre release history.

Version 0.13.5 is live in the Reason Shop. Candidate 0.13.17f1 adds a front settings strip for Remote and halves the piezo’s low thump.

0.13.17f1 Release candidate · 24 November 2026

The piezo’s low thump on each pluck is halved: 6 dB less under 63 Hz and nothing changed from 160 Hz up. The microphones are unchanged.

0.13.16f2 Release candidate · 23 November 2026 · built

Every control 0.13.5 published keeps its Remote name, as Reason requires for an update.

0.13.16f1 Release candidate · 22 November 2026 · built

The device grows from 6U to 7U for a settings strip of editable Oversampling, Piezo Polarity and Width readouts, so each can be mapped with Remote. The sustain pedal stays down when a faded instrument is selected again, and each release reaches the preset that played its note.

0.13.15f3 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Extinguishes the front Note On lamp within 100 ms of releasing keys, completes full Remote mapping and adds Mute/Solo isolation.

0.13.5 Reason Shop release · 17 November 2026 · device 0.13.5f1

Public Reason Shop launch featuring Concert, Classical and Nordic lyres, sympathetic bridge coupling and 9 patches.
