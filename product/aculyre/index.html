Aculyre

Aculyre is a lyre for the Reason rack. All its open strings rest on one bridge and soundbox, so every note you pluck sets the strings around it ringing quietly. Choose a 16-string steel lyre harp, the same harp strung in nylon or a six-string gut lyre, and watch each string light up as it sounds. The lyre

Strings that ring together.

Every string, played or not, ends on one bridge bar standing on the soundbox. Pluck one and the bridge passes its motion to the others, so a phrase leaves a soft halo of strings ringing in sympathy behind it.

  • Three instruments

    A modern 16-string lyre harp in steel, the same frame strung in nylon, and a six-string gut lyre after Anglo-Saxon reconstructions. Switching between them while you play crossfades instead of clicking.

  • The pluck

    Play with a finger, a nail or a pick, and set where along the string and at what angle it is plucked. A pluck near the middle sounds round; one near the bridge sounds bright and thin. No two plucks are quite alike, and each string is tuned by hand a few cents off its note.

  • The hands

    Release sets how long a let-go string rings, from a hand stopping it to letting it die away. Mute rests a palm across the strings, Sympathy sets how freely the unplayed strings answer, and the sustain pedal holds every plucked string.

  • Sharping levers

    With Levers on Auto, a black key raises the lever on the string a semitone below and plucks it, as a lyre harp player sets levers by hand, so a diatonic harp can play in other keys.

  • A pickup under the bridge

    A piezo strip under the bridge bar hears the strings through the wood, with more of their fundamentals and none of the room. The Piezo knob blends from the microphones to the pickup alone, and every patch plays at the same level on Reason’s meter through either.

In the rack

See every string as it sounds.

The six-unit front panel centres on the instrument itself, drawn to scale, with each string lit and widened while it sounds, whether you plucked it or it rings in sympathy. Pluck and lever controls sit on the left; the hands and the output sit on the right. The mod wheel presses the palm mute and the pitch wheel bends every string.

Turn the rack around for oversampling, Note and Gate CV inputs, stereo Width and the stereo output. Automation and Remote mapping connect Aculyre to the rest of your rack.

Reason Rack Extension

Start with Lyre Harp.

Nine factory patches cover the three instruments and ways to play them, from a lyre harp left to ring to a damped steel pick, a picked gut lyre and a stage harp on the piezo alone. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.
