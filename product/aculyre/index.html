The instruments

Open strings, shared bridge and sympathetic shimmer.

Every plucked string transfers energy into the carved soundbox so neighboring open strings bloom naturally behind melodic phrases.

  • Concert, Classical & Nordic

    Switch between a bright 16-string spruce concert lyre, a 7-string tortoise-box classical chelys and an intimate 6-string walnut Germanic/Nordic lyre.

  • Gut, nylon & steel strings

    Swap string materials independently with Finger, Nail, Horn Pick or Wood Pick excitation and adjustable pluck position.

  • Articulations & muting

    Play open plucks, harmonic chimes, plectrum sweeps, palm damp and finger-mute stops with no sample layers.

  • Microphone, piezo & air

    Blend stereo close mic distance, bridge contact piezo and room atmosphere across the mix or discrete rear buses.

In the rack

Per-signal outputs and CV control.

The 6U rear panel provides Main stereo out plus separate Mic, Piezo and Room stems alongside Pitch, Pluck Position, Damping and Body CV inputs.

Reason Rack Extension

Historical and concert tunings out of the box.

Audition 9 factory patches spanning pentatonic Nordic air, ancient gut-strung ensembles and bright modern steel-string ostinatos.
