Oregana

A church organ of 51 stops on three manuals and pedal. Every pipe is a physical model taking wind from bellows that sag and bounce, and a church answers. No samples.

The organ

Every sound is a pipe taking wind.

A flue pipe is a jet of air driving an air column, and a reed pipe a brass tongue beating under a resonator. A key opens a pallet, and the drawn stops’ pipes take their division’s wind. The scales, voicing and winding are typical values and estimates, not measurements of one organ.

  • 51 stops in four divisions

    Hauptwerk, Positiv, an enclosed Récit and Pedal, from one soft stopped flute to full organ with 32′ reeds. Stops slide on and can be left half drawn, six couplers join the manuals, and the Crescendo knob draws the organ up stage by stage.

  • Wind that moves

    Each division has its own reservoir, so a full chord dips the pressure and it bounces back, and every pipe’s pitch and level follow. Run the organ from the blower or the bellows, or switch the wind off and hear it run down.

  • As well kept or as worn as you like

    Detune, Uneven and Action set how far the pipes have drifted, how unevenly they are voiced and how worn the mechanism is. Dead pipes, hanging pallets and leaking channels are there too, and the pitch wheel warms and cools the air so the flues drift against the reeds.

  • A church around it

    Room, Size, Decay and Width set the building, from a chapel to a cathedral, and Seat moves the listener across the nave. The mod wheel is the swell pedal that closes the Récit’s box.

In the rack

One keyboard plays the whole organ.

The keyboard plays the manual you choose and reaches the Pedal with its lowest key or its lowest octaves. Note and Gate CV inputs let Reason’s sequencers play it.

On the back, set the organ’s pitch, the air’s temperature and one of five temperaments, from equal to meantone.

Reason Rack Extension

Registrations and whole organs.

37 patches: 17 registrations, from Principal 8 and Gedackt 8 to Plenum and Tutti, and 20 whole organs in Chapel, Church and Cathedral folders. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.

Version history

Oregana version history.

Unreleased. The latest version is 0.5.0d2; the last version built for Reason is 0.4.0d3, and none is in the Reason Shop.

0.5.0d2 Unreleased · Development version · 13 November 2026 · not yet built

A Seat knob moves the listener across the nave. The flue pipes’ wind noise is calibrated against two recorded organs, and a held flue wavers a little, as a recorded one does.

0.5.0d1 Unreleased · Development version · 13 November 2026 · not yet built

Neighbouring flues pull each other into tune, each division is heard from where it stands, the trumpets are brighter and the engine takes about half the CPU. The pitch wheel warms and cools the air, and every patch’s church is sized to its stops.

0.4.0d7 Unreleased · Development version · 13 November 2026 · not yet built

The stops by family on four paper jambs on a new front, the couplers in the Keyboard card, the mod wheel as the swell pedal with Crescendo a knob of its own, and the 20 whole organs in Chapel, Church and Cathedral folders.

0.4.0d3 Unreleased · Development build · 13 November 2026

Fixes a panel that Reason’s test host refused to draw in 0.4.0d2.

0.4.0d2 Unreleased · Development build · 12 November 2026

The first build for Reason: 20 organ patches after the 17 registrations, and the organ’s front in a green and gilt case over the 51 stops.

0.3.0d1 Unreleased · Development version · 12 November 2026 · not yet built

The first Rack adapter over the Oregana engine: 17 registrations, four panels and Note and Gate CV.
