The organ

Every rank draws real air from the wind chest.

Draw a Principal, add the Octave and the Mixtur to hear the chorus build, then a Trompete to cut through; a stop drawn under a held chord speaks as its slider opens, its pipes drawing on the same wind.

  • 51 stops, four divisions

    The German Hauptwerk and Positiv, an enclosed French Récit with its Voix céleste, Flûte harmonique and Trompette, and a Pedal down to 32 feet, joined by six couplers, with a tremulant on the Positiv and the Récit.

  • Wind and condition

    Wind from a blower or a calcant treading the bellows, with its pressure, unsteadiness and noise; Condition ages the organ, so pipes drift, voicing grows uneven and a worn pallet can let a pipe linger.

  • Playable from one keyboard

    Choose the manual your keyboard plays and let the lowest held note or a split play the Pedal. The mod wheel brings in the full organ in stages, expression works the swell box, and the pitch wheel warms or cools the air.

  • The church around you

    Choose a seat along or across the nave, set the church’s size and width and how long bass and treble ring, and tune to one of five temperaments from equal to meantone.

In the rack

Separate division outputs and tuning trims.

The rear panel carries the main stereo mix and a dry stereo pair for each of the Hauptwerk, Positiv, Récit and Pedal, Note and Gate CV, Pitch, Air temperature and the temperament. 0.14.0 builds add a stereo input to the church and 59 CV inputs, among them one for every stop.

Reason Rack Extension

Seventy-seven registrations ready to play.

Audition chapel continuo flutes, Bach pleno combinations and Romantic cathedral tuttis in the rack, or try the browser edition right now with mouse, keys or Web MIDI.

Rack Extension version history

Oregana release history.

Version 0.12.3, released on 24 November 2026, is the current Rack Extension in the Reason Shop. The latest development build is 0.14.0f1, which adds a CV input for every stop and a stereo input that plays another sound into the organ’s church.

0.14.0f1 Release candidate · 27 November 2026 · built

The release build of the two changes below. The organ itself sounds as before, and songs and patches load unchanged.

0.13.0f1 Development update · 26 November 2026

A stereo Input on the back plays another sound into the organ’s church from where the Hauptwerk stands, through the same Room, Seat, size, decay and width as the pipes.

0.12.5f2 Development update · 26 November 2026

A CV input for each of the 51 stops and both tremulants, CV for the crescendo roller, Swell, wind pressure and Air, and stops drawn as amber jewel-lamp buttons, each lettered to match its CV socket on the back.

0.12.5f1 Release candidate · 22 November 2026 · built

Bounds ciphering notes to settle within 0.75 s when no key is held, adds low-wind harmonic flute speech (canonical 0.12.4), refines treble Vox humana 8′ tuning, smooths reed boot attacks, halts tremulant motion when wind cuts, and turns front Pitch, Temperament and Air readouts into editable Remote displays.

0.12.3 Reason Shop release · 24 November 2026 · device 0.12.3f1

The first Reason Shop release: 51 stops across three manuals and pedal, shared winding, bellows sag and adjustable church acoustics. The Shop gallery shows the 0.12.5f1 panels.

0.12.3f1 Release candidate · 20 November 2026 · built

Matched host master tuning across the full Pitch range and stabilized repeated pipe attacks across host callback sizes.
