Architecture

Circuit models, ring mod and two layers.

BRILLIANCE and RESONANCE change the whole tone at once while each filter keeps its own setting, and the circuits drift and differ from board to board as the rear panel sets.

  • Four sources and a sub

    Saw, pulse and noise enter the filters and sine joins after them, so a pure fundamental stays under any filter setting; five SUB waveforms, independent pulse-width modulation and a swept ring modulator add movement.

  • High-pass and low-pass filters

    Separate resonant high-pass and low-pass filters with key tracking, a filter envelope and an amplitude envelope; velocity can shape level, filter, vibrato and wah.

  • Mono or Double

    Mono plays one layer to both outputs; Double combines two independent layers with adjustable detune and stereo spread, each with 1 to 16 voices.

  • Playing and EXT IN

    Two-semitone bends, vibrato on the mod wheel, an expression pedal on CC11, portamento or semitone glissando, and an EXT IN that takes an external signal in place of the SUB waveform.

In the rack

Engine, variation and EXT IN on the back.

The rear panel holds Mode, Detune, Pan, Voices (1–16 per layer) and Quality, Level, Wah, Expression and the pedal, drift, spread and board tolerances, portamento, the EXT IN input, Note and Gate CV and the stereo output.
