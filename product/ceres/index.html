Ceres

A four-voice polyphonic synthesizer for warm chords, focused bass lines and metallic textures. Ceres models its oscillators, filters, envelopes and ring modulator as circuits rather than playing back samples, and puts every sound control on one hands-on panel. The Rack edition is in development; final specifications will be confirmed at release.

Circuit modelling

Every voice through its own circuit.

Each note runs through modelled oscillator, filter, envelope and amplifier stages. Their state carries on while you edit, so sounds change smoothly as you turn the controls.

  • Mixed sources

    Blend saw, pulse, sine and noise, and set the pulse width. Separate pulse-width modulation adds movement to pulse sounds.

  • High-pass and low-pass filters

    Two filters with independent resonance and keyboard tracking move between rounded fundamentals, hollow colours and bright attacks. A dedicated filter envelope shapes each note.

  • SUB modulator

    Five SUB waveforms add pitch, filter and amplitude movement. Modulation runs freely, inviting evolving sustained notes and rhythmic variation.

  • Ring modulator

    From slow pulses to metallic sidebands. Set its mix and carrier rate, then sweep the carrier with depth, attack and decay as a phrase begins.

  • Velocity response

    Playing strength can change level, filter brightness and vibrato depth, each with its own amount.

  • Sustain I and II

    Sustain I keeps release tails at their original pitch. Sustain II lets free tails follow newly assigned notes.

In the rack

One layer, or two.

Mono uses one four-voice sound layer. Double layers two independent four-voice engines with adjustable detune and stereo spread. Turn the rack around to choose the mode; the patch saves it.

The six-unit front panel shows a value readout under every control. Stereo outputs, automation and Remote support connect Ceres to the rest of your rack.
