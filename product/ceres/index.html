Circuit-modelled polysynth · Reason Rack Extension

Ceres

Warm basses, expressive plucks and dreamy pads, shaped by the interactions of modelled oscillators, filters, envelopes and amplifiers. Ceres brings the CS-50-inspired circuit approach into Reason with up to sixteen voices per layer, a second sound layer in Double mode and a focused bank of 23 patches.

  • 1–16 voices per layer
  • 23 Rack patches
  • Mono or Double

Circuit modelling

Every voice through its own circuit.

Each note runs through modelled oscillator, filter, envelope and amplifier stages. Their state carries on while you edit, so sounds change smoothly as you turn the controls.

  • Mixed sources

    Blend saw, pulse, sine and noise, and set the pulse width. Separate pulse-width modulation adds movement to pulse sounds.

  • High-pass and low-pass filters

    Two filters with independent resonance and keyboard tracking move between rounded fundamentals, hollow colours and bright attacks. A dedicated filter envelope shapes each note.

  • SUB modulator

    Five SUB waveforms add pitch, filter and amplitude movement. Select EXT to use an audio signal connected to the rear EXT IN socket as the modulation source. Internal modulation runs freely.

  • Ring modulator

    From slow pulses to metallic sidebands. Set its mix and carrier rate, then sweep the carrier with depth, attack and decay as a phrase begins.

  • Velocity response

    Playing strength can change level, filter brightness and vibrato depth, each with its own amount.

  • Sustain I and II

    Sustain I keeps release tails at their original pitch. Sustain II lets free tails follow newly assigned notes.

  • BRILLIANCE and RESONANCE

    Two front-panel levers reshape the filters together for broad tonal changes as you play. Rear controls add glide, expression-pedal response and voice variation.

  • Choose your processing quality

    Use 1x, 2x or 4x processing, with 1x as the default. The rear voice selector sets one to sixteen voices for each layer; Init starts with four.

In the rack

One layer, or two.

Mono sends one sound layer to both outputs. Double mixes two independent layers with adjustable detune and stereo spread. Each layer supports one to sixteen voices; Init selects four. Turn the rack around to choose the mode, voice count and quality, all saved with the patch.

A six-rack-unit panel keeps the synthesis controls in reach. Rear EXT IN brings another rack signal into modulation, while stereo outputs, automation and Remote support connect Ceres to the rest of your session.

Reason Rack Extension

Twenty-three starting points.

Three warm basses, three expressive plucks and three dreamy pads sit alongside Init and thirteen Classic Estimates. The complete classic set covers String I/II, Brass I/II, Flute, Electric Piano, Clavichord, Harpsichord, Guitar I/II and Funky I/II/III.

Classic sounds are labelled Estimate because the Rack controls cannot reproduce every internal factory setting. They are interpretations of the original preset table, with no claim of a measured hardware match.

Final stereo-linked sample-peak protection keeps overloads at −1 dBFS, with immediate attack and 50 ms recovery. Hard notes or chords can reduce gain; it adds no latency or automatic makeup gain.

Rack Extension version history

Ready for its first release.

The 1.0.3f1 candidate is built for the first public release. Reason Shop acceptance and publication are pending; Ceres is not yet available to purchase.

1.0.3f1 23 October 2026 · First-release candidate

Every patch, Init and Reset Device now drifts by 1.8 cents and spreads its voices by 0.5 cents. Output trims are lowered where loud chords drove the peak guard, so factory chords keep their headroom.

The filter reuses more of its calculations, with the tested engine output unchanged.

1.0.0f1 23 October 2026 · Earlier candidate

Circuit-modelled synthesis with saw, pulse, sine and noise sources, resonant high-pass and low-pass filters, envelopes, PWM, SUB and ring modulation, velocity response, BRILLIANCE and RESONANCE levers and rear EXT IN modulation.

Includes the latest accepted 23-patch bank: thirteen labelled Classic Estimates, nine modern basses, plucks and pads, plus Init. Their sound settings and output trims are preserved. Mono and Double modes support one to sixteen voices per layer and 1x, 2x or 4x processing; Init selects Mono, four voices and 1x.

Reuses pitch and filter calculations to reduce processing work while preserving the tested engine output exactly. Native measurements do not establish a Reason CPU-meter improvement. Stereo-linked output protection follows the completed signal path.
