Four churches

A stone church, from the nave floor to the organ loft.

Each space couples a nave and choir with a tall crossing vault, piers and transept, so the reverb blooms from early reflections into a long, even decay the way a real church does.

  • Chapel, Church, Basilica & Cathedral

    Four cruciform spaces with decay times from 1.6 to 7.5 seconds at the centre setting.

  • Organ Loft, Distance & Seat

    Raise the source from the floor into the west gallery, move it along the nave, and set the listener’s seat from one aisle to the other.

  • Freeze

    Hold the live harmonic cloud indefinitely, even armed from silence to catch the next chord, and let it go back into its natural decay.

  • Duck & protection

    An external stereo sidechain ducks the tail by up to 24 dB, and a stereo-linked peak limiter guards the output.

In the rack

Sidechain and sixteen CV inputs on the rear.

Patch stereo audio in and out, send a kick or a dry vocal into the Sidechain inputs, and modulate Decay, Loft, Seat, Freeze, Mix and eleven more controls from CV, each with its own attenuator.

Reason Rack Extension

Sixty-four patches in six folders.

Start from Church Nave at 30 % Mix, or browse Spaces & Geometry, Aux Sends, Organ & Choir, Vocals & Keys, Drums & Acoustics and Ambient.
