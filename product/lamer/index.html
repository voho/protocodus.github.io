From subtle to spectacularly lossy

Real perceptual codec degradation, live.

Instead of bitcrushing samples blindly, Lamer runs genuine MP3 and Ogg Vorbis encoders in real time so transients, pads and noise erode authentically.

  • MP3 & Ogg Vorbis

    Switch between stepped MP3 pre-echo/metallic shimmer and smoother Ogg Vorbis spectral smear from 256 down to 8 kbps.

  • Cascaded generation loss

    Repeat encoding 1 to 4 times in series to stack generational artifacts cleanly under one Mix knob.

  • Filters & crossfade

    Low-pass and high-pass filters shape what the encoder hears, and Bitrate Crossfade glides between bitrates for smooth automation at a higher CPU cost (on the back in 1.0.0, on the front in 1.0.1f3 builds).

In the rack

2U effect with mono and stereo routing.

Patch stereo or mono audio: one cable in gives mono or dual-mono out, two give stereo. The back holds the Bitrate Crossfade switch, which glides between bitrates instead of stepping through them. There are no CV inputs.

Reason Rack Extension

Drop on a drum bus or synth channel.

Automate Bitrate through a transition, or stack two rounds under a parallel Mix blend for lo-fi character on a drum bus or synth channel.
