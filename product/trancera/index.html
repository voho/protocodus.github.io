Virtual analog synth · Reason Rack Extension (in development)

Trancera

An eight-voice virtual analog polysynth with a seven-saw super wave, hard sync, ring and cross modulation, a multimode filter and an analog output stage, running inside at 88.2 kHz.

  • Seven-saw super wave
  • Unison & arpeggiator
  • 160 factory patches
In development Explore Trancera ↓

Rack Extension in development. Source 0.9.6f6 (28 November 2026) is on Rack main, its build pending; the last build is 0.9.6f5. Not yet in the Reason Shop. Version history.

Oscillators & filter

Two oscillators, one super wave.

Oscillator 1 has seven waves, among them a seven-voice detuned super wave and a feedback oscillator; Oscillator 2 adds hard sync, ring and cross modulation, all into a –12 or –24 dB multimode filter.

  • Unison, Mono & Legato

    Polyphonic Unison, Mono eight-voice Unison with detune and stereo spread, or legato mono with portamento.

  • Arpeggiator

    Five modes over one to four octaves, synced to Reason’s tempo, with Hold and a variable gate.

  • Two LFOs

    Four waves each, fade-in, tempo sync and key reset, with LFO 2 depth on the mod wheel or aftertouch.

  • Effects & delay

    Thirteen effects including chorus, flanger, phaser and overdrive, five delay modes with tempo sync, and a switchable converter and analog output stage.

In the rack

A CV bay with twelve inputs and four outputs.

Drive Note, Gate, the mod wheel, aftertouch, cutoff, resonance, oscillator controls, cross mod, balance, LFO 1 rate and amp level from CV, and send both LFOs and both envelopes out to the rest of the rack.

Reason Rack Extension

160 patches in eight folders.

Start from the Super Saw Lead default, or browse Bass, Brass & Strings, Keys & Organs, Leads, Pads, Plucks & Arps, Sync, X-Mod & FX and Vintage, twenty sounds in each.

Rack Extension version history

Trancera version history.

In development for Reason; source 0.9.6f6 (28 November 2026) opens up the panel, and its build is pending. 0.9.6f5 was built but not uploaded.

0.9.6f6 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

The open panel: the blue body is lit from above with the trance supersaw printed across it, and the cards are smoked glass it shows through, with soft shadows and lit top edges. Lit keys get a hot white lamp, and Oscillator 1’s Super key shows the plain saw.

0.9.6f5 Development build · 28 November 2026 · built, not uploaded

The panel is restyled in the Protocodus blend: a flat blue body, black cards, white legends and orange lamps and keys. The controls and sound are unchanged.

0.9.6f4 Release candidate · 25 November 2026 · built

The final 9U front panel, with waveform icon pads, Ring under Sync on Oscillator 2 and a Mix & Pitch bay.

0.8.0d1 – 0.9.6f2 Development builds · 23–25 November 2026

Filter Velocity, aftertouch routing, Polyphonic and Mono Unison with stereo spread, Delay tempo sync, overdrive, Oscillator 2 noise, a tempo-synced LFO 1 with key reset, the switchable converter and output stage, the rear CV bay and the 160-patch library.
