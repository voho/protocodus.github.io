The crossfader

More than a fade between two sources.

Linear, Equal Power, Smooth and Square Root set two gains. The transitions go further, and each is deepest at the middle of the fade, so a fade resting at either end plays plain A or plain B.

  • Seven transitions

    Spectral Wipe, Bass Swap, Width Morph, Pan Swap, Distance, Duck Handoff and Adaptive. Bass Swap crosses the highs first and the kick and bass last; Duck Handoff dips each source under the other’s hits.

  • The level holds

    A level guard keeps the output close to an equal-power fade all the way across: in the tests every transition stays within 1.5 dB of it.

  • Stereo or mono

    Each input pair also accepts a mono source, and left and right stay separate throughout the crossfade.

  • Smooth under automation

    Slew of up to 50 ms smooths changes in CV, position or curve. Automate MIX, Curve, Slew and CV Amount to build transitions into an arrangement.

In the rack

See each curve before you use it.

Each key on the five-unit panel draws its curve or effect, and its tooltip says what it does. A live scope shows the actual A and B gains at the current position, beside input and output meters.

A CV indicator shows when external control takes over, and a rear-panel amount control attenuates the incoming CV. Remix sits with the Utilities and has no bypass, because a crossfader is always mixing.

Reason Rack Extension

Connect A and B.

Send two sources to A and B, turn MIX, then try the transitions. Remix has no presets; its four controls are saved in your song.
