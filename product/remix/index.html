The crossfader

Every fade shape on one 3U utility.

Blend untouched A at left to untouched B at right, with clicks eliminated even on fast CV transitions.

  • Shape, Skew & Sigmoid

    Turn Shape from exponential dip through linear to logarithmic overlap, bias transitions toward A or B with Skew, and ease endpoints into an S with Sigmoid.

  • Normal, Constant Power & Gain

    Hold overlapping energy steady for uncorrelated stems (Constant Power) or coherent sources (Constant Gain), or use unnormalized Normal curves.

  • Smooth automation & CV

    Apply up to 50 ms of control smoothing so rapid CV swings and slow fades move cleanly without steps.

In the rack

Stereo or mono routing with trimmed CV.

Patch two stereo or mono sources into A and B on the 3U rear panel; cabling Position CV overrides the front Main fader through Reason’s trim knob.
