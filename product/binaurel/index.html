3D headphone space

Beyond left and right panning.

Combine interaural timing, pinna elevation spectral cues, near-field air damping and early reflections to move sounds behind, above or close beside the listener.

  • Azimuth, elevation & distance

    Spin sources smoothly through 360°, lift above or below the horizon, and pull instruments forward from intimate proximity into deep room space.

  • Mono focus or Stereo Orbit

    Anchor a tight point source or spread stereo inputs into a counter-rotating pair whose width narrows naturally as distance grows.

  • Parametric room & dry blend

    Shape room reflections, absorption and output spread, or blend back cleanly with an Output Mix knob.

In the rack

Six CV inputs for orbital motion.

Patch LFOs, envelopes or sequencers into Azimuth, Elevation, Distance, Room, Mix and Stereo Width on the 4U rear panel with per-input trim controls.

Reason Rack Extension

Automatic PDC and mono safety.

Reports its 18 ms spatial look-ahead buffer automatically to Reason and folds cleanly with low phase coloration.
