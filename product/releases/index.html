Development releases · 6 October 2026

The latest instrument builds.

The 6 October build covers Linux x64 VST®3 and standalone editions of all five plug-in instruments, plus CLAP for YouKnow, Electry and Taikor. These are development packages. The macOS and Windows refreshes are pending; the Rack changes are awaiting SDK builds and validation in Reason.

What changed

Sound and playing.

  • YouKnow 1.2.0

    The current circuit profile brings interacting waveform-mixer loading, nominal original-module filter tuning, temperature-dependent amplifier control and revised chorus motion and transfer. The first five changes were selected by ear; unmeasured module and chorus coordinates remain named estimates.

    New instances start in six-voice Original timing. Saved Direct sessions retain Direct. The output amplifier’s antialiasing raises latency to 120 samples below 176.4 kHz and retains 41 at higher rates. Main NOISE keeps more upper-frequency energy at lower quality settings.

    Full YouKnow release history ↗

  • Acustra 1.3.2 · source only

    The source at 8ea776e7, 12 October 2026, follows the 1.0.0 review build above. Acustra now plays one guitar body: the Bellido 1978 model is removed, and a session that chose it plays the Original. The panel adds the Sustain switch and the Release Noise and Room knobs; a new instance starts with Release Noise at 10% and Piezo Mix at 20%. Strum direction follows the host tempo, and keyswitches C0, C#0 and D0 (MIDI notes 24–26) latch downstrokes, upstrokes or the automatic rule. Only the tuning’s range sounds, from the lowest open string to the twentieth fret of the highest; pitches above it no longer play natural harmonics.

    Measured dips at each body’s air mode and strongest mid-band mode change the microphone sound; nobody has listened to the 1.3.1 and 1.3.2 dips yet. Fixes from two MIDI audits cover Gather Chords, channel-mode messages, bypass and host reset. No 1.3.2 package has been built for release.

    Full Acustra release history ↗

  • Electry 1.2.0

    A fresh build of the current eight-string guitar, amps and effects. This build adds no sound or control changes.

    Electry release history ↗

  • Septum 0.9.0

    A fresh build of the current two-tone synthesizer, with its updated interface spacing. Ten voices, Single/Dual/Split modes and the seven-saw oscillator remain available.

    Septum source and build notes ↗

  • Taikor 1.0.0

    A fresh build of the current four-drum ensemble, including front and rear heads, shell and air interactions, performer variation and room convolution. This build adds no sound or control changes.

Reason Rack Extensions · Unreleased source

Shared instruments in the rack.

Acustra and YouKnow are thin Reason adapters over their canonical instruments. Maremba owns its full instrument here. On 6 October all three universal build attempts stopped because the licensed Jukebox SDK was unavailable, and none of the following changes had a new Rack binary or Reason validation. Acustra’s entry is current to 12 October.

  • YouKnow · after 1.2.0f1

    The adapter now compiles the current canonical DSP directly and selects its active circuit profile. Chart/direct MIDI and CV timing, the 1–16-voice control and the Rack’s output calibration remain. Latency follows the current 120/41-sample rule. Twenty-six factory headroom pads are lowered by 0.22–2.00 dB; musical controls and Init are unchanged.

  • Acustra · 1.2.11f1 source

    1.2.8f1 is the last candidate built with the Jukebox SDK and created in Reason Recon. The 1.2.9f1 to 1.2.11f1 source adds the Sustain switch and a Room knob, starts Body at 82% and pins the canonical 1.3.2 instrument with its strum keyswitches. It has not been built with the SDK or tested in Reason yet.

  • Maremba · after 1.4.0f6

    The Kalimba’s box receives signed bridge forces from its yielding tine modes. Partials near bridge nodes now transmit less force to the board. The established fundamental level, all bar models and stored patch controls are preserved.
