  • YouKnow 1.3.0

    Refined oscillator, filter, amplifier and BBD chorus modelling, clearer high-frequency noise and revised voice variation. Explore 100 patches with up to 16 voices, Poly 1, Poly 2 and Unison, tempo-synced modulation and eight CV inputs.

    Four physically modelled instruments with softer wooden attacks, natural Decay at the midpoint and lighter, diffuse rooms. Blend Close, Room and Piezo, color the Main mix with Drive, and use independent direct outputs for separate processing.
