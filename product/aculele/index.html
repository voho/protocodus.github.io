Aculele

Aculele is a ukulele for the Reason rack, built on the string and body model behind Acustra. Pick a soprano, concert or tenor body, nylon or fluorocarbon strings and standard, Low G or D tuning, then strum it with a finger, pick a melody or play it with the thumb. The ukulele

Four short strings on a small body.

Each string is modelled as a stiff, vibrating string that loses energy and drives the bridge and the body. Change the body, the strings or the way you play, and the attack, the brightness and the length of each note change with it.

  • Re-entrant tuning

    Standard tuning puts a high G on the fourth string, so chords sit close and bright. Low G swaps in a lower fourth string, and D tuning raises every string a whole tone.

  • Strings and bodies

    Nylon and fluorocarbon sets each have their own gauges, tension and damping. Soprano, concert and tenor bodies follow published dimensions and scale lengths, in spruce, mahogany or maple. Their upper resonances are spread as densely as those counted on a real ukulele top, so high notes no longer catch on a single harsh resonance.

  • Strum, pick, thumb or finger

    Strum sweeps a finger over the neck, where a ukulele is strummed: down with the nail, back up with the pad. Finger and thumb pluck with soft skin, and the thumb strums back up on its nail; the pick gives melodies a sharper edge. Keyswitches latch downstrokes, upstrokes or automatic strumming, and holding F0 turns each stroke into a muted chuck that also stops strings left ringing by the sustain pedal.

  • Mic, piezo and room

    Hear it through a stereo mic pair, a mono mic or an under-saddle piezo, then set the body, room and width. A separate piezo output carries the pickup on its own for its own processing.

  • Ready for the mix

    Every patch plays at the same level on Reason’s meter, and the bodies, woods and captures stay within about 1.5 dB of it, so you can compare sounds without riding the fader. A limiter on the main and piezo outputs keeps the hardest chords out of the red.

In the rack

Choose the ukulele. Then play it.

The front panel chooses the instrument on the left (body, wood, strings and tuning) and plays it on the right: technique, pluck position, touch, palm mute, string age and capture. A fretboard shows where each string is playing and how strongly it rings.

Turn the rack around for oversampling, bend range, detune, release noise and the piezo’s blend polarity. A stereo main output, a direct piezo output, Note and Gate CV inputs, automation and Remote mapping connect Aculele to the rest of your rack.

Reason Rack Extension

Start with a strum.

Seven factory patches cover the three bodies and tunings, from a concert strum and a soprano in D tuning to tenor fingerstyle and a stage piezo sound. Play chords from any keyboard: Aculele chooses a string and fret for each note, in the shapes of a ukulele chord book, and a three-note strum still sounds all four strings. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.
