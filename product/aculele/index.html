The instrument

Four strings, three bodies and island woods.

Strums follow the hand’s down-up swing, no two plucks land quite alike, and a finger moving along a string slides to its new fret.

  • Three sizes, four woods

    Soprano, concert and tenor bodies with spruce, mahogany, koa or cedar tops, nylon or fluorocarbon strings, in Standard, Low G or D tuning.

  • Finger, thumb, pick or strum

    Touch, Position, palm Mute and String Age shape each note, and a chord from one keyboard finds a playable four-string voicing that favours open strings in first position.

  • Strokes, chucks and hand gestures

    C0, C♯0 and D0 latch downstrokes, upstrokes or the automatic swing, F0 holds muted chucks, and F♯0 to A0 brush the strings, shift the fret hand, tap the body or damp every string.

  • Microphones or piezo

    A stereo pair, a mono microphone or the under-saddle piezo, with Body, Piezo blend, Room and Width, and a Direct Piezo output that carries the pickup on its own.

In the rack

A direct piezo output and the keyboard map.

The rear panel holds Oversampling (1x, 2x or 4x), Bend range, Detune, Release noise and the piezo blend’s polarity, the main stereo output, a mono Direct Piezo output and Note and Gate CV, with a map of the keyswitches, hand gestures and controllers.

Reason Rack Extension

Seven ready-to-play patches.

Open Concert Strum, Soprano D Tuning, Tenor Fluorocarbon Fingerstyle or Low G Tenor, then change the body or the playing hand to hear the same part on another ukulele.

Rack Extension version history

Aculele release history.

Version 0.4.70 is live in the Reason Shop, and its build 0.4.70f6 is also the latest on Rack main.

0.4.70 Reason Shop release · device 0.4.70f6

Softer thumb plucks, upstrokes balanced across the four strings, track Mute and Solo that fade ringing notes, and a Note On lamp that follows the keys. The device grows to 9U for a settings strip whose readouts can be mapped with Remote.

0.4.70f3 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Turns off the front Note On lamp cleanly when notes release (even as nylon rings out), with full Remote mapping and Mute/Solo silence.

0.4.64 Reason Shop release · 17 November 2026 · device 0.4.64f2

Public Reason Shop launch featuring Soprano, Concert and Tenor bodies, High-G/Low-G tunings, Chord Mode and 7 factory patches.
