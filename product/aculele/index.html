Aculele

Soprano, concert and tenor ukuleles simulated from nylon or fluorocarbon strings on koa, spruce or mahogany bodies, with strumming, picking and chord recognition.

  • Three body sizes
  • High-G or Low-G

The instrument

Four strings, three bodies and island woods.

Switch re-entrant High-G or linear Low-G tuning alongside thumb, felt or nylon pick attacks—all generated live with sympathetic coupling.

  • Three sizes, two string sets

    Choose bright Soprano, balanced Concert or warm Tenor bodies with curly koa, spruce or mahogany tops and nylon or crisp fluorocarbon strings.

  • Pick, fret or strum chords

    Auto mode assigns melody notes cleanly; Chord Mode maps held triads and sevenths to dedicated downstroke, upstroke, chuck and mute keys.

  • Expressive techniques

    Play hammer-ons, pull-offs, natural harmonics, slides and rhythmic hand slaps across the strings or soundboard.

  • Condenser mic & piezo

    Mix stereo room microphone placement with under-saddle piezo attack inside the main mix or route them to discrete mixer channels.

In the rack

Direct stems, capo and CV modulation.

Route Main out or tap separate Mic, Piezo and Room outputs on the 8U rear panel beside Quality, Detune, Capo and Vibrato/Mute CV inputs.

Reason Rack Extension

Seven ready-to-play patches.

Open Soprano Island Strum, Concert High-G Fingerpick or Low-G Tenor Warm and customize string age, body resonance or strum timing live.
