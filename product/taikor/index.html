Physically modelled taiko

Taikor

Four taiko drums, four strokes and up to eight players per note. Play a single drum or an ensemble from your MIDI keyboard, with every hit synthesised from the drum model.

  • Four drums
  • Four strokes
  • 1–8 players per note

For macOS, Windows and Linux. VST3, CLAP and standalone, with Audio Unit on macOS.

The instrument

Play the drum. Shape the ensemble.

Sixteen notes give you direct access to the four drums and their strokes, without switching articulations.

  • Four distinct drums

    Ō-daiko, nagadō-daiko, okedo-daiko and shime-daiko, each with its own size and construction. Adjust the diameter, head tension and materials.

  • Four strokes per drum

    Play Don, Edge, Muted and Rimshot across the 4 × 4 grid. Each row selects a drum; each column selects a stroke.

  • One player or eight

    Layer up to eight independent drum models per note. Ensemble Variation changes the companions’ timing, placement and drum character.

  • A responsive strike

    Velocity controls the stick’s impact speed. Adjust bachi hardness, strike position and Humanise to shape the attack and variation between hits.

  • Damping and pitch

    Use the modulation wheel to damp a ringing head and the pitch wheel to bend a sounding stroke. MIDI controls can also move the strike position.

  • Microphone perspective

    Set mic distance, spread and stereo width to change how the drums sit in the output. Add drive or use the low cut when needed.

In your setup

One drum, or the whole family.

Use Drum Layout to choose one drum retuned across the rows or four drums at their own sizes. Select a performer identity for each instance when layering parts, then save the setup in your DAW project or a host preset.
