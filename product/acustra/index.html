Physically modelled acoustic guitar

Acustra

An acoustic-guitar instrument whose every note is synthesised in real time from six physical string models, a passive measured bridge and a measurement-derived body, behind a panel of ten controls.

Inside the instrument

Six strings,
and only six.

MIDI first selects one of six strings, each covering its open note plus twenty frets, so chords, repeated notes and still-ringing strings are constrained the way a guitar is.

01

Nothing is sampled

Each note is generated as it plays, from six string waveguides driving a 50-mode measured bridge and 96 measured body modes. The plug-in contains no sample player and no recorded note audio; the reference recordings link only into the offline fit renderer and its tests.

02

Six strings, twenty frets

The allocator replucks a note on the string still sounding it once its key is up, prefers a free string requiring the lowest valid fret, and steals deterministically only when every playable string is occupied. A group of three or more notes arriving on one sample is swept as a strum, low to high then back, so consecutive strums alternate; every pluck draws its own point within 0.02 of the string length either way, so a repeated stroke is a new take rather than a copy.

03

Both hands on the strings

CC2 is continuous bridge-hand pressure, darkening the note as it shortens it, and is an exact no-op at zero. CC68 is the fretting hand: while it is down, a note a sounding string can already reach is hammered on rather than replucked, releasing it pulls off to whatever the hand still holds, and note-off velocity decides whether the finger stays on the string or lifts and leaves the open string ringing.

04

Stereo from the guitar itself

The 96 body modes carry independent complex residues that reproduce the two microphone paths of one measured guitar: close microphones placed 10 cm above the top plate and 20 cm apart, one each side of the bridge. The stereo image comes from body radiation rather than artificial delay or reverb.

What it does not do

Stated plainly.

Apart from bridge-hand damping, legato, natural harmonics and the fretting hand’s strike and lift, version 0.1 is sustain-only: fret and finger noise, string squeak, buzz, artificial and pinch harmonics, body knocks, pick direction, tapping and slapping are absent, the bridge radiates one transverse string axis rather than two, fret collision is not modelled, and MPE is lower-zone only. Shape and Body Material morph one measured flamenca-body model rather than separate measurements of four guitar sizes or four woods, and no controlled expert study has compared this build with real guitars or leading commercial instruments.

Measured bridge and body coefficient tables are adapted from Robert Mores’ archive under CC BY 4.0, and the DAFx-26 EJ45 construction data is used under CC BY 4.0. The FreePats, Shinyguitar and Eastman E1D recordings are dedicated under CC0 1.0 and are not linked into the plug-in.
