Inside the instrument

Strings, bodies and real playing techniques.

Six strings drive a bridge and a body adapted from measurements of real guitars, so every note follows from where the string is plucked, how hard, and with a finger, a pick or the thumb.

  • Four bodies, three woods

    Parlor, Auditorium, Dreadnought and Jumbo with Spruce, Mahogany or Maple tops, in Standard, Drop D, DADGAD or Open G, retuned by string tension as on a real guitar.

  • Finger, pick or thumb

    Touch and Position shape each stroke, String Age takes the strings from fresh and bright to worn and dull, and no two plucks land quite alike.

  • Strums and palm mutes

    Three or more notes strum down and up as a hand does; C0, C♯0 and D0 pin the direction, F0 and F♯0 strum the chord you hold, and Mute or CC2 rests the heel of the hand on the strings.

  • Microphones or piezo

    A stereo pair, a mono microphone or the under-saddle piezo with a modelled preamp; Piezo blends the pickup under the microphones, and Body, Stereo and Room set how they hear the guitar.

In the rack

A direct piezo output and the keyboard map.

The rear panel holds Oversampling (1x, 2x or 4x), Detune and the piezo blend’s polarity, the main stereo output, a mono Direct Piezo output and Note and Gate CV, with a map of the keyswitches, the guitar noises and the controllers.

Reason Rack Extension

Eight patches, from Dreadnought Steel to palm mutes.

Open Dreadnought Steel, Parlor Steel Fingerstyle, Open G Slide-ready or Palm Muted Steel Pick, then switch the guitar, the wood or the picking hand to hear the same part another way.

Rack Extension version history

Acustra release history.

Version 1.9.41 is live in the Reason Shop. Candidate 1.9.65f1 is built, and source 1.9.69f1 adds hand-shaped fretting for live chords and legato lines and fuller picked notes.

1.9.69f1 Release candidate source · 27 November 2026 · source only

A chord change played legato is fretted where the hand is, so the new chord no longer lands in a shape no hand holds. Carries 1.9.68f1’s changes, which were never built on their own.

1.9.68f1 Release candidate source · 26 November 2026 · source only

Chords played live are fingered as one hand holds them, legato lines stay where the hand is, strummed chords ring less around 1.2 kHz, and picked notes regain their body in the low mids.

1.9.65f1 Release candidate · 25 November 2026 · built

String Age ages each wound string by its measured share, the low E most and the D and G least.

1.9.64f2 Release candidate source · 23 November 2026 · source only

Songs keep their Oversampling: a new Saved choice opens 1.9.0 songs at the rate they stored, and it still cannot be automated.

1.9.64f1 Release candidate · 22 November 2026 · built

Keyed strums share frets, and every current parameter can be mapped with Remote from the front panel.

1.9.63f5 Release candidate source · 21 November 2026 · source only

Turns the front Quality and Detune readouts into editable value displays so both map directly with Edit Remote Override Mapping alongside Piezo Polarity.

1.9.63f4 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Includes full Remote mapping on every control, Mute/Solo silence, key-following Note On lamp and refined 1.9.15–1.9.50 string/body acoustics.

1.9.41 Reason Shop release · device 1.9.41f2

The current Reason Shop version, with the instrument engine at 1.9.41.

1.9.0 Earlier Reason Shop release

Added four selectable guitar bodies, improved string dispersion and palm mute transitions, building on the 1.3.2 debut (20 October 2026).
