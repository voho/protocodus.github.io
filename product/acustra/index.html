Physically modelled acoustic guitar

Acustra

Six steel strings, a measured bridge and a resonant guitar body. Play fingerstyle, picked lines or strummed chords from your MIDI keyboard, then choose microphones, an under-saddle piezo or a blend of both. Every note is synthesised in real time, without recorded note samples.

  • Six steel strings
  • Three picking styles
  • Five tunings

In development. VST3 and standalone for macOS, Windows and Linux, with Audio Unit on macOS.

The instrument

Six strings. One resonant body.

Each pluck starts a physical string model. The six strings share a measured bridge and guitar body, so ringing strings respond to the next note you play.

  • Finger, pick or thumb

    Choose three distinct ways to play the strings. Move the pluck position, soften or firm up the touch, and use velocity to shape each attack.

  • Chords that become strums

    Notes arriving together are swept across the strings as alternating downstrokes and upstrokes. Gather Chords combines slightly staggered keyboard notes into one strum, with 30 ms of added latency.

  • Shape and material

    Explore Parlor, Auditorium, Dreadnought and Jumbo body shapes, with Spruce, Mahogany and Maple material directions. Start from a construction preset, or choose the measured Bellido 1978 model.

  • Five tunings and string age

    Play in Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, Open G or Half-step down. Fresh or aged strings change brightness and decay, while the tuning changes the notes each string can reach.

  • Expression under your hands

    Use the modulation wheel for vibrato and CC2 for bridge-hand damping. Bend with the pitch wheel, or use lower-zone MPE for individual string bends and pluck position.

  • Microphones and piezo

    Choose stereo microphones, a mono microphone or an under-saddle piezo through its modelled preamp. Blend the piezo beneath the microphones, or send it to a separate output for your own processing.

In your setup

Choose the guitar. Find your touch.

Start with Guitar, Model, Picking and Capture, then adjust the body, string age and playing controls. Keep the microphone sound, blend in the piezo, or process the two captures separately in your DAW. Save the complete setup with your project or a host preset.
