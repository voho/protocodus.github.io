Inside the instrument

Strings, bodies and real playing techniques.

Every string vibrates cleanly with stiff-string partials, fretboard damping and sympathetic resonance across one shared bridge and body.

  • Four guitar bodies

    Choose Dreadnought, Concert, Jumbo or Parlor—each with its own soundhole resonance, bracing timbre and pick-to-bridge strike position.

  • Play lines or strum chords

    Auto voice allocation places notes intelligently across the six strings; Chord Mode strums held shapes with dedicated downstroke, upstroke and mute keys.

  • Articulations & human feel

    Trigger hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, natural harmonics and palm mutes via velocity or keyswitches, with subtle string buzz and finger noise.

  • Dual microphone & piezo

    Balance cardioid condenser microphone placement (neck to lower bout) against under-saddle piezo attack inside the main mix or on separate outputs.

In the rack

Direct microphone and piezo routing.

Route Main stereo or pull Mic, Piezo and Room outputs independently on the rear panel, alongside Capo, Quality, Detune and CV inputs for Vibrato, Mute, Body Resonance and Stereo Width.

Rack Extension version history

Acustra release history.

Version 1.9.0 is live in the Reason Shop. Candidate 1.9.63f4 (and source 1.9.63f5) expands sympathetic coupling, chord detection and Remote controls.

Turns the front Quality and Detune readouts into editable value displays so both map directly with Edit Remote Override Mapping alongside Piezo Polarity.

1.9.63f4 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Includes full Remote mapping on every control, Mute/Solo silence, key-following Note On lamp and refined 1.9.15–1.9.50 string/body acoustics.

1.9.0 Current Reason Shop release

Added four selectable guitar bodies, improved string dispersion and palm mute transitions, building on the 1.3.2 debut (20 October 2026).
