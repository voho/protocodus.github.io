The machines

Five architectures, converter to tank.

Anti-aliasing filters, quantized converters and machine sample rates sit around each delay tank—from 12-bit 1976 grit to 16-bit 1986 breadth.

  • Five machines (1976–1986)

    Pick 1976, 1979, 1981, 1985 or 1986. Knobs adopt each machine’s real steps and ranges.

  • Four programs

    Switch Reverb, long Space, gated Non-Lin and Origin (the 1976 tank fed through later converters); unavailable controls dim cleanly.

  • Six tactile knobs & live meter

    Shape Decay, Low, Mid, High, Filter and Predelay while monitoring input clipping, four tank loops, reflections and outputs in seconds, kHz and ms.

  • Click-free automation

    Every parameter sweeps smoothly without zipper noise, zero latency, and machine or program changes crossfade cleanly.

In the rack

Quad outputs on the back.

Machines 1976 and 1985 feed a secondary rear stereo pair (Ch III and IV) alongside the main outs so two mixer channels can wrap a quad hall around the mix.

Reason Rack Extension

Patch onto an aux send.

Opens on 1976 Hall ready for drums, vocals or synths; audition the five eras, trim Input before CLIP lights, or blend dry/wet inline.
