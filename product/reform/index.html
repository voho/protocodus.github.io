Sound morpher · Reason Rack Extension (in development)

Reform

Two sounds in, one sound out. Mix sets how far the sound has gone from A to B: half way is one sound with a pitch, timbre and level between the two, not a crossfade.

  • Two stereo inputs
  • Mix with CV
  • Five Follow knobs & Dynamics
In development Explore Reform ↓

Rack Extension in development. Source 0.1.0d12 (28 November 2026) is on Rack main; the last build is 0.1.0d10. Not yet in the Reason Shop. Version history.

Morph

Not a crossfade, not a vocoder.

Each input is taken apart into its level, its spectral envelope and its fine structure, and the output is put together from parts that each stand somewhere between A’s and B’s.

  • Mix is the master

    From 0 % input A to 100 % input B, played by hand, stepped or driven by CV without clicks.

  • Five Follow knobs

    Timbre, Pitch, Amp, Attack and Noise each run with Mix at the middle, are held at A at the left end and held at B at the right.

  • Timbre moves, not mixes

    A formant at 700 Hz becoming one at 1.2 kHz passes through 900 Hz instead of fading out at one place and in at another.

  • Dynamics

    At 0 % the two inputs are weighed by Mix alone. Turned up, the louder input at each moment gives more of the texture and the timbre, so drums into a synth keep the drums on the hits and give way to the synth between them.

  • Live display

    A’s spectrum becoming B’s, with the sound Reform plays standing between them, drawn from the engine’s own readings.

In the rack

Two stereo inputs and a Mix CV on the rear.

Reason inserts Reform on input A; cable input B by hand. One cable in a pair plays on both sides, and the Mix CV input has its own trim.

Reason Rack Extension

Latency compensated by Reason.

Reform works one frame behind, under 50 ms at every sample rate, and reports it to Reason, which lines it up with the rest of the song.

Rack Extension version history

Reform version history.

In development for Reason; source 0.1.0d12 (28 November 2026) lets Dynamics weigh the timbre too. 0.1.0d10 is the latest SDK build, created fresh in Reason’s validation host but not uploaded.

0.1.0d12 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

Turned up, Dynamics weighs each input’s timbre by its level too, so a drum’s quiet, bright tail no longer brightens a synth between the hits. At Dynamics 0 the sound is unchanged.

0.1.0d11 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

About a third less CPU for bit-identical output, with a frame’s work spread over every batch it may use. Nothing that plays changed.

0.1.0d10 Development build · 28 November 2026 · built

Drums no longer drag a synth’s pitch around: the pitch moves only as far as both sounds are sure of it, so a snare’s noise moves nothing, and drums morphed with a synth lose most of their grain.

0.1.0d9 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

The panels gain depth: a gold-to-violet body with grain and the morph’s ridges printed on it, a lit header and cards on soft shadows.

0.1.0d5 – 0.1.0d8 Development updates · 28 November 2026 · 0.1.0d8 built

The morph keeps each sound’s own detail instead of an MP3-like wash, a new Dynamics knob lets the louder input give more of the texture, a pitched sound no longer jumps half a period at onsets, and a quiet input’s texture stays as quiet as it is.

0.1.0d4 Development build · 28 November 2026 · built

Fixes 0.1.0d3 stopping in Reason with “Value out of range” as soon as sound reached it: the display’s numbers now fit their properties.

0.1.0d3 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

A is gold and B violet everywhere on the panels, and every card has a lamp and a title. Nothing that plays changed.

0.1.0d2 Development update · 27 November 2026 · source only

Fewer words on the panel, and a frame’s work spread over three of Reason’s batches, so the slowest batches cost about a third less.

0.1.0d1 Development build · 27 November 2026 · built

The first device: two stereo inputs, a stereo output, Mix with a CV input and trim, five Follow knobs and the live display.
