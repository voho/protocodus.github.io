Acukoto

A 13-string paulownia koto built from physical physics: movable ji bridges, Hirajoshi or Chromatic tuning, left-hand presses and sympathetic resonance across every string.

The instrument

Silk strings, paulownia body and movable bridges.

Every string drives the hollow paulownia soundboard through an adjustable bridge so unplayed strings shimmer softly in sympathy.

  • Two tunings, 13 movable bridges

    Play traditional Hirajoshi scale patterns (with per-bridge fine shift) or switch Chromatic to map every note directly across the keyboard.

  • Left-hand techniques & bends

    Keyswitches trigger Oshide pitch presses, Tremolo picks, Keshi damped plucks, Sukui-zume upstrokes, Suri-zume string scrapes and open glissandi.

  • Physical string & plectrum controls

    Dial plectrum size, sawari buzz, silk string damping and release decay with zero sampled wave loops.

  • Microphone, piezo & body room

    Mix stereo overhead condenser capture with bridge piezo punch and acoustic room reflections.

In the rack

Latching keyswitches, CV and stems.

On the rear 6U panel, check the full latching keyswitch legend, tap independent Mic and Piezo stereo outs, and modulate Pitch, Left Hand Press and Expression via CV.

Reason Rack Extension

Play traditional or modern parts.

Load any of the 9 factory patches—from bright Concert Hirajoshi to soft damped Chromatic parts—or sweep the mod wheel for expressively bent melody phrases.

Rack Extension version history

Acukoto release history.

Version 0.25.23 is live in the Reason Shop, and its build 0.25.23f2 is also the latest on Rack main.

0.25.23 Reason Shop release · device 0.25.23f2

Released notes stop on the stretch the hand plucked, latched techniques stay on through a tuning change, Oversampling can be mapped with Remote from a front readout, and the redrawn back lists every technique key with what it does. A gate that falls with a pitch change no longer rings an extra string.

0.25.22f2 Release candidate · 18 November 2026 · built

Stops released notes via a realistic point contact near the ryukaku bridge, keeps latched techniques through tuning changes and widens rear LATCHING KEYS captions.

0.25.21f4 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Verifies full Remote control mapping, key-following Note On lamp and sequencer Mute/Solo isolation.

0.25.16 Reason Shop release · 12 November 2026 · device 0.25.16f1

Published in the Reason Shop with sympathetic string coupling, bridge placement controls, Mic/Piezo outputs and 9 factory patches.
