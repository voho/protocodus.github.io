Acukoto

Acukoto is a koto for the Reason rack: thirteen silk strings, each on its own movable bridge, plucked with ivory picks. Play a note a step above a string and the left hand presses that string up before the pluck; the strings you leave alone ring along softly. The Rack edition is in development; final specifications will be confirmed at release.

The koto

Bend into the notes the tuning leaves out.

There are no recordings inside Acukoto. Each string is computed as a stiff silk string, and every string drives one hollow body with an arched top, walls and two holes in its back.

  • The left hand

    A key a semitone or a tone above a string presses it up behind its bridge. Play a single line legato and the press slides to the next note without a new pluck.

  • Two tunings

    Hirajoshi, tuned as six professional players tuned it, with its slightly narrow semitones, or hankumoijoshi.

  • Every key sounds

    Turn the press off and a key between the strings moves a bridge instead. Every key from the piano’s lowest A to its top C sounds at its own pitch.

In the rack

See every string as it sounds.

The six-unit panel draws the koto from above with each bridge standing where the tuning puts it, and lights each string as it sounds: held, ringing after release, ringing in sympathy or pressed by the left hand.

The mod wheel rests a palm on the strings. Turn the rack around for Note and Gate CV inputs and the stereo output. Automation and Remote mapping connect Acukoto to the rest of your rack.

Reason Rack Extension

Nine patches to start from.

Nine factory patches cover the two tunings and ways to play them. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.
