Acukoto

A modelled koto: thirteen silk strings on movable ji over a hollow paulownia body, plucked with ivory tsume. Play a step above a string and the left hand presses it up, and the lowest keys play the koto’s techniques.

  • 13 silk strings
  • Hirajoshi & hankumoijoshi
  • 17 technique keys

The instrument

Silk strings, paulownia body and movable ji.

Each string rings into the hollow paulownia body, and the strings you leave alone ring along softly with the ones you play. There are no recordings inside.

  • Two tunings, oshide presses

    Hirajoshi, tuned as six professional players tuned it, or hankumoijoshi. Oshide presses a string up for a key a step above it; Legato slides the press to the next note, and Off moves a ji instead.

  • Tsume or finger

    Pluck with the ivory picks or the bare finger, 2 to 12 cm from the bridge, and press the last plucked string up a whole tone or relax it with the pitch wheel for slow bends and vibrato.

  • Seventeen technique keys

    Ten latching keys, among them sukui-zume, tremolo, pizzicato, the octave harmonic and staccato, change the notes that follow; seven more play tsuki-iro, yuri, oshi-hanashi, glissandi and the chirashi and suri scrapes at once.

  • Body hits and the room

    Twelve keys under the lowest string strike the body with a palm, a knuckle or a rap. Release, Mute and Sympathy shape the ringing; Room, Width and Volume shape what leaves the device.

In the rack

The keyboard map on the back.

The rear panel maps the keyboard’s four zones, names all seventeen technique keys and what the wheels and the sustain pedal do, and holds Note and Gate CV, Oversampling (1x, 2x or 4x) and the stereo output.

Reason Rack Extension

Nine patches, from tsume to finger.

Open Koto Hirajoshi, Koto Hankumoijoshi, Damped Tsume, Finger Koto or Wide Hall Koto, then move Position along the string or switch Oshide.

Rack Extension version history

Acukoto release history.

Version 0.25.23 is live in the Reason Shop, and its build 0.25.23f2 is also the latest on Rack main.

0.25.23 Reason Shop release · device 0.25.23f2

Released notes stop on the stretch the hand plucked, latched techniques stay on through a tuning change, Oversampling can be mapped with Remote from a front readout, and the redrawn back lists every technique key with what it does. A gate that falls with a pitch change no longer rings an extra string.

0.25.22f2 Release candidate · 18 November 2026 · built

Stops released notes via a realistic point contact near the ryukaku bridge, keeps latched techniques through tuning changes and widens rear LATCHING KEYS captions.

0.25.21f4 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Verifies full Remote control mapping, key-following Note On lamp and sequencer Mute/Solo isolation.

0.25.16 Reason Shop release · 12 November 2026 · device 0.25.16f1

Published in the Reason Shop with sympathetic string coupling, bridge placement controls, Mic/Piezo outputs and 9 factory patches.
