FM synthesis

Two operators, fully in reach.

Clear keys, nasal basses, bowed tones and shifting voices all come from one modulator driving one carrier. Every part of that pair is editable.

  • Modulator and carrier

    Set each operator’s frequency ratio, detune, level and waveform. Feedback and FM amount move the tone from pure sine to bright, buzzing spectra.

  • Per-operator envelopes

    Attack, two decay stages and release give each operator its own shape, so the brightness of a note can change independently of its volume.

  • Modulation and expression

    An LFO with delay drives pitch and amplitude modulation. Velocity response, pitch bend, the modulation wheel and sustain support expressive playing.

  • Output circuit and chorus

    A modelled output stage, stereo chorus, Drive, Width and a tone filter colour the result. On the back, take the sound from the preamp, AUX or phones stage, and run AUX or phones on ideal power, a low battery or AC. Choose original pitch behaviour or equal tuning.

  • Double

    Switch on Double and two independent units play each note, one on each side, with their own spread and detune.

In the rack

Main controls in front. Detail behind.

The eight-unit front panel keeps the main sound and performance controls within reach, with pitch and modulation wheels and numeric readouts on every knob. Turn the rack around for detailed operator settings, modulation configuration, circuit choices and fine calibration.

Stereo outputs, Note, Gate, FM and Level CV inputs, automation and Remote mapping connect Press78 to the rest of your rack.

Rack Extension version history

Press78 release history.

The Reason Shop released version 0.1.13 on 2 November 2026. The latest Rack candidate is 1.0.4f1.

1.0.4f1 Rack Extension candidate · 3 November 2026

Loud FM operators saturate at the top of their range. Glide and vibrato delay follow the chip clock more closely, and voices settle differently as notes decay.

1.0.3f1 Development build · 3 November 2026

The bank contains 150 original sounds in ten category folders, plus Init. Includes fifteen Vintage voices; the 1.0.3 version advances the release metadata without changing the 1.0.2f3 sound bank or DSP.

Reason Shop publication is separate from this development build.
