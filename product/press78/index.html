Synthesis

Direct FM without deep menu trees.

The front keeps the main sound and performance controls in reach, with blue modulator and pink carrier controls and wave buttons that show their waveform; key scaling, sensitivities, polyphony, portamento and the output stage sit on the back.

  • Two-operator FM core

    Two operators with their own frequency ratios, waveforms and envelopes, plus feedback, detune, velocity response and modulation, on up to 12 voices with pitch bend, modulation and sustain.

  • Double and the chorus

    Double adds a second, independent unit with stereo spread and detune; the Real profile starts with the original pitch, the recorded stereo chorus and gentle clock drift, or choose Equal pitch and the Studio chorus.

  • The output circuit

    Choose the Preamp, AUX or Phones output, run AUX and Phones from ideal power, a low battery or an AC adapter, push more level into them with Stage drive, and shape the result with Tone, Drive and Width.

  • 150 original presets

    Fifteen in each of ten folders, brass, bass, lead, pluck, keys, pad, strings, effects, percussion and vintage home-keyboard sounds, each with its own velocity and wheel response, plus Init.

In the rack

CV inputs and rear setup controls.

The rear panel holds key scaling and sensitivities for both operators, the LFO, polyphony, portamento, the clock and chorus, the output stage and its power, and Note, Gate, FM and Level CV beside the stereo output. 1.0.6 builds gather the output and tuning controls in a Setup column.
