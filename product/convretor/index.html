The converter

Let one sound move another.

Filter the kick out of a drum loop and let it push a filter, or use a vocal’s peaks to open a pad. At the default scale, a unity audio peak reaches full CV.

  • Four independent outputs

    Set each output anywhere from 0 to 3x and use Inv to reverse its polarity.

  • Filters before the detector

    Optional high-pass and low-pass filters choose the part of the sound that drives the CV. A live scope draws the band that reaches the detector.

  • Unipolar or Bipolar

    Choose a positive peak level, or keep the polarity of the strongest audio peak. Input Gain runs from 0 to 4x before filtering and detection.

In the rack

Input to outputs, left to right.

The compact three-unit panel follows the signal from the input through the filters and peak conversion to the four numbered outputs, with an input meter and signed CV indicators at each stage.

Automate Input Gain, LP, HP, Scale and Invert as your arrangement develops. The Unipolar/Bipolar choice is a manual setting saved with your song.

Reason Rack Extension

Patch audio in, CV out.

Convretor has no presets; its controls are saved with your song.
