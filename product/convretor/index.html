Audio to CV

Turn audio dynamics into four rack modulations.

Isolate a kick or the bright hits of a loop with the filters, follow every peak or smooth it into a swell with Attack and Release, and drive filters, levels or sequencers anywhere in the rack.

  • Band-limited detector

    Input Gain from 0 to 4x, then low-pass and high-pass filters at 6, 12 or 24 dB per octave choose the part of the sound that drives the CV, with a live scope of the band.

  • Envelope follower

    Attack from 0.1 ms to 1 s and Release from 1 ms to 10 s, or instant at zero. Unipolar gives a positive level; Bipolar keeps the polarity of the strongest peak.

  • Gate on every hit

    The gate opens when the follower crosses Threshold (−60 to 0 dB) and closes 3 dB lower after 30 ms, so a held low note does not chatter; a Trigger key opens it by hand.

  • Four independent outputs

    Each output has its own Scale from 0 to 3x and an Inv switch, so one signal can move several parameters at different strengths and polarities.

In the rack

One audio input, four CV outputs and a gate.

Cable a track, a send or a spider split into the mono Audio input on the 5U back, then route CV 1 to 4 and the Gate output anywhere in the rack or a Combinator. CV updates once per 64-sample batch, and a gate shorter than a batch still reaches its output.

Reason Rack Extension
