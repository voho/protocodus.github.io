Inside the instrument

Four acoustic percussion voices, simulated per strike.

Every velocity recalculates the mallet impact against arched wood bars or spring-steel tines coupled to brass tubes, gourd cavities or boxed bodies.

  • Four modelled instruments

    Concert Marimba (5 octaves), Bass Marimba (3.5 octaves with deep tube boom), Balafon (18 pentatonic bars with mirliton buzz membranes) and Kalimba (17 steel tines with finger clicks).

  • Mallet & strike physics

    Move between rubber, wool and wood mallets, adjust strike position from centre to node, and switch to Two-mallet hand alternating variation.

  • Live bar & gourd resonators

    Tune bar damping, tube or body resonance, ambient noise and sympathetic buzz per bar rather than static EQ curves.

  • Humanization & room

    Shape per-note tuning drift, velocity response and a parametric spatial room built around the instruments.

In the rack

Seven stereo outputs and CV control.

Run the full kit through the main stereo mix or tap direct stems for Low Wood, High Wood, Tubes, Body, Air and Key Noise alongside Pitch, Expression and Resonance CV inputs.
