Inside the instrument

Four acoustic percussion voices, simulated per strike.

A soft stroke and a hard one differ in more than volume, the keys you don’t strike ring along with the ones you do, and the balafon’s membranes buzz on firm strokes.

  • Four modelled instruments

    Imperial Rosewood pairs concert-marimba bars with tuned brass tubes, Mayan Padauk has shorter-ringing bars over cedar boxes, Balafon Ancestral has fire-cured keys over gourds, and Kalimba Artisan plucks steel tines on one wooden box.

  • Mallet, thumb and strike

    Yarn, Cord, Rubber, Wood or the mallet’s shaft for the bars, and flesh, thumb, nail, pick or a fingernail tap for the kalimba; Hardness, Position and Decay shape the attack and the ring.

  • Rolls, release and tunings

    The mod wheel rolls held notes with two alternating mallets, or lowers a palm over the kalimba’s sound hole; Ring, Damp and Dead decide what a released key does, in equal temperament or six traditional African tunings.

  • Close, room and pickup

    A close stereo pair heard through a modelled head, a Wood, Chamber or Hall room and a contact pickup, with Width; Drive, Warmth and Limit shape the main mix.

In the rack

Seven outputs and seven CV inputs.

The rear panel carries the main stereo mix, Close and Room pairs and a mono Piezo output that bypass the mix’s drive and limiter, Note and Gate CV with Hardness, Position, Coupling, Sympathetic and Volume CV, and Tune, Detune, Scale, Voices, Quality (1x to 8x) and the limiter’s timing.

Rack Extension version history

Maremba release history.

Version 1.6.5 is live in the Reason Shop. Its gallery now shows build 1.6.5f4, which is also the latest on Rack main.

1.6.5f4 Reason Shop build · 23 November 2026 · built

Quality stays on the back’s 1x to 8x switches and gains an editable readout in the front header, so it can be mapped with Remote but never automated. The sound is unchanged.

1.6.5f3 Release candidate · 17 November 2026 · built

Makes Quality remotable on the rear and front wordmark readout, verifies the key-following Note On lamp and sequencer Mute/Solo isolation, with identical sound to Shop 1.6.5.

1.6.5 Current Reason Shop release

Added Bass Marimba, Balafon and Kalimba alongside Concert Marimba, seven outputs and expanded resonator modelling.

1.4.0 – 1.5.7 First Shop releases · 13 October 2026 onward

Launched Maremba in the Reason Shop and refined modal damping, two-mallet response and factory presets.
