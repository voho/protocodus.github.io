Suppression

Cuts resonances, keeps the body of the track.

Thirty-two self-tuning parametric EQ bands in Normal and Ahead modes—or linear-phase spectral suppression in Linear mode—track narrow build-ups across voice, drums, guitars or full mixes.

  • Normal, Ahead & Linear modes

    Normal tracks dynamically with 0 ms minimum-phase EQ; Ahead previews look-ahead frames to catch sharp transients; Linear runs zero-phase overlap-add suppression with automatic PDC.

  • Depth, Sharpness & Selectivity

    Control cut depth, filter Q focus and sensitivity to solo whistled resonances vs broad harshness, shaped by draggable low/high shelves and two bell emphasis nodes.

  • Stereo Balance & Sidechain

    Switch L/R or Mid/Side, bias sensitivity continuously with Balance (−100% L/M to +100% R/S), cross-link channels or carve tracks from an external sidechain key.

  • Delta listen & output character

    Audit only the removed ringing via Delta, protect punch with a zero-pre-echo Hit gate, and finish with Clean, Iron, Tube or Opto level-matched stages.

In the rack

External sidechain and CV modulation on the rear.

Patch stereo main audio, send a competing vocal or snare into Sidechain In, and modulate Depth, Sharpness, Selectivity, Balance or Emphasis Frequency via CV on the 8U rear panel.

Reason Rack Extension
