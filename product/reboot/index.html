The machines

Hardware register timing and real chip limits.

Every pitch stems from the machine’s real period table, and envelopes write registers once per video frame exactly as a classic sound driver did.

  • Handheld, Console & expansions A–E

    Switch machines, add any of five Console cartridge chips on Cart, listen raw at the chips (Pin) or through the analog board stage (Line).

  • Parts, shapes & frame envelopes

    Play Pulse, Triangle, Wave, Noise or cartridge channels framed by calibrated Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release faders over a live waveform plot.

  • Poly, Mono, Arp & Double

    Glide smoothly in Mono, cycle chords frame-by-frame in Arp, layer up to 8 boards, and pan a detuned second set left/right in Double mode.

In the rack

Machine path, Boards and Note/Gate CV.

On the 8U Broadcast Desk rear panel, configure Boards (1–8), Cart channels, Console Bias, 1×–8× oversampling, stereo outs and Note/Gate CV inputs.

Reason Rack Extension

Eight patches from Square Lead to Console chords.

Audition raw Handheld duty sweeps, Console triangle basses and thick cartridge multi-board pads immediately in Reason.
