The machines

Hardware register timing and real chip limits.

Every pitch stems from the machine’s real period table, and envelopes write registers once per video frame exactly as a classic sound driver did.

  • Twelve sounds, one list

    The Handheld’s Pulse, Wave and Noise, the Console’s Pulse, Triangle and Noise, and six cartridge sounds from expansions A to E. Shape picks the pulse width, wave or noise mode, and a green scope draws what plays; Pin hears the chips alone, Line adds the machine’s output stage.

  • Driver or chip envelopes

    Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release are written by the driver a step at a time, or handed to the chip’s own envelope unit, looping where the chip can loop; Sweep and Pace play the pulses’ pitch sweeps.

  • Poly, Mono, Arp & Double

    Up to eight machines play at once (six by default), a chord filling one machine’s free channels before taking another. Mono glides, Arp steps through held keys, Sync ties both and the vibrato to Reason’s tempo, and Double plays a second set of machines on the right.

In the rack

Machine setup, signal path and CV.

The 8U rear panel draws the live signal path and holds Boards (1–8), Cart channels, Console Bias, 1×–8× oversampling, the stereo outputs, Note and Gate CV and six modulation CV inputs: Pitch, Fine, Shape, Level, Mod and Vibrato.

Reason Rack Extension

Eight patches from Square Lead to Console chords.

Audition raw Handheld duty sweeps, Console triangle basses and thick cartridge multi-board pads immediately in Reason.

Rack Extension version history

Reboot version history.

In development for Reason; source 0.10.0f3 (28 November 2026) lights the panels from above, and its build is pending. 0.10.0f2 was built but not uploaded.

0.10.0f3 Development update · 28 November 2026 · source only

The panels are lit from above: a graded body and rail with a light grain, and cards that cast soft shadows and have lit top edges. Colours, controls and layout are unchanged.

0.10.0f2 Development build · 28 November 2026 · built, not uploaded

The panels are restyled in the Protocodus blend: a flat slate body, dark cards with a lit lamp, and one colour per meaning, green for the chip, orange for the voice and magenta for modulation.

0.10.0f1 Release candidate · 27 November 2026 · built

Boards default to six, so every one of the twelve sounds plays six-voice chords out of the box, and Poly chords fill a machine’s own free channels before taking another unit.

0.9.0f1 Release candidate · 24 November 2026 · built

One Sound list of twelve keys replaces Board, Cart and Part, Glide can sync to the tempo, Volume no longer clicks, and Oversampling gains a front readout for Remote.

0.7.0d1 – 0.8.0d1 Development updates · 23 November 2026

Each machine is a unit with its own crystal, so Double plays two real units left and right; nine new controls including Envelope, Sweep, Chord and Spread; and Arp and Vibrato rates that sync to Reason’s tempo.

0.5.2d1 Development build · 20 November 2026 · built

Refines Mono pedal releases, Poly pitch-bend channel fallback and live Arp tracking, shares oversampling across matching channels, and completes the walnut/aluminium Broadcast Desk front and rear layouts (from 0.5.1d2).

0.2.3f1 – 0.4.0d2 Earlier builds · November 2026

Unified the instrument around the Handheld and Console machines with Cart expansions A–E, time-based ADSR faders, Double stereo panning and CV input routing.
