The machines

Nothing in Retra is a recording.

Clippers solve real diode equations, vinyl grooves bend with stylus geometry, speakers push a nonlinear moving cone, and MP3/Ogg run genuine codecs down to 8 kbps.

  • Eight stages in any order

    Swap Distort, Player (cassette/vinyl), Wobble (deck/turntable), Loss, Noise, Amplifier, Speaker and Digital via slot steppers.

  • Reactive analog noise

    Hiss blooms around notes, vinyl crackle thickens on loud highs, hum rides mains frequency and radio static drifts with signal level via Freq and Follow.

  • True codecs & moving speakers

    Switch bit-crushed converters, live MP3 or Ogg Vorbis streams, and nonlinear radio, cab, phone or horn speaker cones.

  • Unified Amount scaling

    Every character dial follows one intuitive curve from subtle 20% patina to 100% disintegration under master Amount, Tone and Mix.

In the rack

Chain signal flow and Quality oversampling.

Switch individual stages and 1×/2×/4× analog oversampling while subtle per-channel component differences add organic width to mono sources on hardware stages.

Reason Rack Extension

Rack Extension version history

Retra version history.

In development for Reason; build 0.8.0f5 (26 November 2026) turns the chain into eight slots, each choosing its own stage from a drop-down, with every control mappable with Remote.

0.8.0f5 Release candidate · 26 November 2026 · built

Bigger round On keys, and a filler circle where a stage uses fewer knobs, so every card keeps four.

0.8.0f4 Release candidate · 26 November 2026 · built

Every slot control now appears under Edit Remote Override Mapping, whichever stage the slot holds.

0.8.0f3 Release candidate · 25 November 2026

A new device is silent in silence: the default patch’s tape hiss now follows the signal.

0.8.0f1 Release candidate · 24 November 2026

Eight slots instead of eight stages in an order: each card’s header is a drop-down of the eight stages and Empty, so every slot can be mapped with Remote and automated.

0.7.1f2 Release candidate · 21 November 2026 · built

Makes every stage icon and circled slot-order number Remote-mappable alongside the six stage model fixes and SIMD optimization introduced in 0.7.1d1 (Static high-band stability at 44.1 kHz, Crush mono symmetry, clean Crackle clicks, accurate Vinyl RIAA/2×/4× treble and 1× top octave on Amplifier, Distort and Tape).

0.7.0f6 Release candidate · 11–17 November 2026 · built

Added front Quality buttons, named Remote controls, Wobble machine models and 25 factory patches.
