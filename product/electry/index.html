The guitar

Six strings, magnetic pickups and wiring physics.

Nickel strings vibrate across balanced magnets whose output is shaped by passive Volume, Neck Tone, Mid Tone and cable capacitance before driving Reason’s amps.

  • Three pickups & five positions

    Switch Bridge, Bridge+Mid, Middle, Mid+Neck or Neck directly on the diagram; Volume and two tone circuits load pickups authentically.

  • Pick position, attack & feel

    Slide the pick 25–250 mm along the string, dial Pick gauge, Strum spread (up to 40 ms), finger-lift Release and player Feel.

  • 15 keyswitches & string solo

    Keys C0–D1 switch Dead notes, Legato, Slides, Natural/Pinch Harmonics, Whole/Half Bends, Vibrato, Stroke direction, Fingers and Damp; C-1 to F-1 solo specific strings (F#-1 clears).

  • Tremolo arm, palm mute & pedal

    Use Pitch Wheel as floating tremolo arm or finger bend, Mod Wheel for palm mute, Aftertouch for vibrato and Sustain Pedal to ring notes cleanly.

In the rack

Direct instrument signal for your amp rig.

Unamplified single-coil output feeds Reason’s Softube Amps, Scream 4 or external pedals via Mono Audio Out, with Pitch, Expression and Lookahead routing on the rear.

Reason Rack Extension

Seven dry voicings built to hit an amp.

Open Sparkly Neck Clean, Quack Pos-4 Funk or Bridge Lead Bite directly before your favourite cabinet simulation or pedalboard.

Rack Extension version history

Electry version history.

In development for Reason; build 2.11.2d1 (25 November 2026) adds a Telecaster beside the Strat, a keyswitch deck on every piano key, measured pickups and strums that ring on. The panels shown are from 2.10.0f1, the last build with new panels.

2.11.2d1 Development update · 25 November 2026

With Fingers latched, a chord is plucked from its lowest string up whatever stroke was latched before.

2.11.1d1 Development update · 25 November 2026

Every patch plays at about −11 VU, and the output limiter now starts at −0.5 dBFS, out of the way of normal playing.

2.11.0d1 Development update · 25 November 2026

Measured Strat pickups with more resonance from 4 to 7 kHz, and the Telecaster’s own bridge and neck pickups. Every patch is set to one level.

2.10.0f1 Release candidate · 24 November 2026 · built

A harder strum is a faster strum. Builds 2.7.0d1 to 2.10.0d1, gathered here, add a Telecaster beside the Strat with its 3-way switch and fixed bridge, chord changes on the right strings, strums that ring on into the next chord, and a peak limiter.

2.6.2f1 Release candidate · 23 November 2026

Strat model fixes for rubs, slides, harmonics and pull-offs, lower CPU use, and the Clear key retired now that string solos last only while held.

2.6.0f1 Release candidate · 22 November 2026

A keyswitch deck that uses every piano key, a master Tune, saddle lamps that light while a string is soloed, and the pitch wheel bending a string by default.

2.5.1d2 Development build · 19 November 2026 · built

Adds front string-solo indicator lamps captioned C-1 through F-1 past the nut (with F#-1 clearing held solos) building directly on 2.5.1d1 string-solo voicing.

2.5.0f1 Development milestones · November 2026

Introduced the 8U fretboard panel, five-way pickup selector, 15 keyswitched articulations and modeled passive tone stack.
