Articulations & tone

Control the performance.

Strings resonate together, picking varies between strokes, and a modelled fretting hand chooses where each note is played along the neck.

  • Seven playing styles. Sustain, palm mute, hammer-on / pull-off, natural harmonic, pinch harmonic, slide and dead note. Hold a keyswitch briefly or latch the style for a whole phrase.
  • Pick direction. Choose down, up or alternate strokes independently of the playing style. Hold the tremolo keyswitch for automatic repeated picking at an adjustable rate.
  • Bends, vibrato and feedback. Use the pitch wheel for bends, a held keyswitch for vibrato and the modulation wheel to push an amplified note into sustained feedback.
  • Pickups and strings. Choose single-coil or humbucker pickups. Adjust pick position, pick hardness, string age and guitar construction to change the attack and tone.
  • Mono, Stereo or Double. Keep one centred guitar, spread its strings across the stereo field, or use two separately modelled performances, one on each side.
  • Individual string triggers. Hold a chord and repick its strings from MIDI or the on-screen fretboard to add rhythmic patterns and accents.
Workflow

Amps, effects and factory rigs.

Four factory rigs cover a dry guitar, Drop-E metal, muted DI and blues rock lead.

Choose American Clean, British Crunch or Modern High-Gain, then add distortion, compression, delay and room ambience. Set all five effect amounts to zero for a dry signal you can run through your own amp and effect plug-ins.
