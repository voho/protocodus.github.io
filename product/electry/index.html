The instrument

Shape the way each note is played.

From palm-muted rhythm parts to bends and sustained leads, the playing controls are part of the instrument.

  • Seven playing styles

    Sustain, palm mute, hammer-on / pull-off, natural harmonic, pinch harmonic, slide and dead note. Hold a keyswitch for a moment or latch a style for the phrase.

  • Pick and repick

    Choose down, up or alternate strokes. Hold a chord and repick individual strings, or use the tremolo keyswitch for repeated picking.

  • Bends, vibrato and feedback

    Bend with the pitch wheel, hold a keyswitch for vibrato, and use the modulation wheel to bring in sustained feedback.

  • Pickups and string character

    Blend neck and bridge pickups, move between single-coil and humbucker character, and adjust pick position, hardness and string age.

  • Mono, stereo or double

    Keep the guitar centred, spread its strings across the stereo field, or use two separately modelled performances for a doubled part.

  • Three amp voices

    Choose American Clean, British Crunch or Modern High-Gain. Add drive, compression, delay and room ambience.

In your setup

Start dry. Bring in the rig.

Factory Default starts with a dry guitar. Choose Drop-E Metal or Blues Rock Lead for a different starting point, then switch FX on to hear the rig. The FX switch bypasses the effects without losing their settings.
