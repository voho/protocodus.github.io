Physically modelled guitar

Electry

An eight-string dry electric guitar built from an original physical model, where each string is its own waveguide voice and a fretting hand decides where in the neck a phrase actually lands.

Inside the instrument

One voice
per string.

No guitar performances, sample libraries, neural network weights or third-party presets are included. The only bundled third-party audio-derived data is a CC0 1,024-sample cabinet-impulse prefix behind a default-off comparison build.

01

Eight modelled strings

One voice per physical string on a 22-fret eight-string tuned E1-B1-E2-A2-D3-G3-B3-E4, playable from E1 to D6. A fretting hand with a position and a four-fret shape allocates each note to a string, replacing a rule that always chose the lowest available fret and so made most of the fretboard unreachable.

02

Two keyswitch banks

One bank latches the pick stroke, down, up or alternate; the other sets the play style, from sustain and mute to hammer-on and pull-off, natural and pinch harmonics, slide and dead note. Any of the twenty-one combinations is reachable in at most two keyswitches. Two further keys are held rather than latched: A♯0 adds fretting-hand vibrato while a stopped note rings, and B0 runs tremolo picking at 4 to 20 strokes per second.

03

Pedal, amp and room

Five amount controls plus an Amp Voice selector give a distortion pedal, three amplifier and cabinet paths, compression, a 360 ms lead delay and a stereo room. Every amount defaults to zero, where it is a bit-exact dry bypass, and the nonlinear stages run inside an up-to-8x oversampled domain.

04

One shared bridge

All eight strings terminate on the same saddle: the ones you are not fingering ring off it, and the ones you are exchange energy through it, set by the Sympathetic Ring control. The modulation wheel raises that coupling and opens a feedback path from the amplified output back into the strings, so with the amp or distortion up a held note regenerates into self-sustaining feedback, while a dry DI never can.

What it does not do

Stated plainly.

Body Size, Shape and Construction are exact no-ops. Channel pressure, polyphonic aftertouch, MPE CC 74 timbre and Note Off velocity are deliberately unassigned, pitch control is not a physical vibrato-bar simulation, and the amplifier and speaker paths are parametric response models rather than cabinet impulse responses, loudspeaker mechanics, microphone or room captures. Electry is unreleased, and its 28-parameter layout is a development layout that makes no compatibility promise to earlier snapshots.

Electry does not claim to be a capture-accurate clone of any one instrument, and its three amplifier paths are bounded circuit-derived families rather than capture-accurate replicas of named amplifiers, individual power-tube specimens, microphones or rooms. Demo files 18 to 22 are original Electry studies, not endorsements or artist sound-alikes.
