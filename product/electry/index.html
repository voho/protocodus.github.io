Physically modelled guitar

Electry

An eight-string Drop-E electric guitar, synthesised from the strings, bridge and pickups. Play palm-muted riffs, chords and expressive leads from your MIDI keyboard, with hands-on control of picking, fretting and a dry DI or built-in amp rig.

The instrument

Put the pick and fretting hand in your control.

Choose the strings, change the contact and let the shared bridge carry sympathetic ringing through the guitar.

  • Seven playing styles

    Move between Sustain, palm mute, hammer-on / pull-off, natural harmonic, pinch harmonic, slide and dead note. Hold a style keyswitch over a gesture or latch it for the phrase. Continuous palm pressure can damp any style.

  • Choose a string. Pick it again.

    Use down, up or alternate strokes, and solo individual strings with keyswitches. Repick a held chord string by string from MIDI or the live fretboard, or hold the tremolo keyswitch for repeated picking at an adjustable rate.

  • Bends, vibrato and feedback

    Bend a whole chord with the pitch wheel, or use MPE pitch bends for individual strings. Hold the vibrato keyswitch over a fretted note. With the amp or pedal engaged, the modulation wheel can bring the strings into sustained feedback.

  • Pickups, strings and build

    Select the neck, bridge or both pickups, shape their single-coil-to-humbucker character and adjust tone, pick position, hardness and string age. Guitar Build combines walnut-to-ash body character with six light and heavy string setups.

  • Mono, stereo or double

    Use Mono for a centred DI, Stereo for one guitar's divided-pickup image, or Double for two independently performed guitar models. The shared bridge lets played and unplayed strings ring together.

  • Three amp voices, dry by default

    Choose American Clean, British Crunch or Modern High-Gain, with pedal drive, compression, delay and room ambience. The FX switch bypasses the whole rig while retaining its settings; Standard and High choose the effects' processing quality.

Inside the model

From a moving string to an amplifier circuit.

Each note comes from interacting string, hand, bridge and pickup models. The optional rig continues that signal through circuit-derived pedal and amp stages, with distinct speaker and cabinet voices.

  • Strings in two directions

    Each of the eight strings vibrates in two modelled directions. Scale length, gauge and tension set its stiffness and overtones; frequency-dependent losses shape the ringing tail. Pick-induced tension adds a subtle sharp attack that settles as it decays. Fretting changes the speaking length, decay and pickup response.

  • Pick, finger and fret contact

    The pick has a finite contact width and an asymmetric release, with stroke-to-stroke variation shared across a strum. Finger touches select natural and pinch harmonics from the vibrating string. Slides preserve that vibration while winding friction supplies the scrape; adjustable contact noise and fret/saddle artifacts add texture.

  • Two hands and a shared bridge

    Palm mute and dead note use distinct bridge-hand and fretting-hand losses; fretted palm-muted notes tighten as the speaking length shortens. Bridge motion drives sympathetic ringing in the other strings. Three body resonances informed by a controlled walnut-and-ash study add build character, alongside six curated scale and gauge setups.

  • Magnetic pickup behaviour

    Pickup position, finite magnetic aperture and one- or two-coil sensing shape the string signal. Modelled flux nonlinearity, induced voltage and loaded coil resonance then give the pickup its electrical response. The passive tone control moves and damps that resonance.

  • Three circuit-derived amp families

    Published valve models drive the 12AX7 preamp stages. American Clean and British Crunch add passive tone-stack circuits, distinct phase splitters and 6L6GC or EL34 power-tube models. Grid loading, overload recovery, supply sag, negative feedback and transformer saturation shape their response; Modern High-Gain supplies a separate cascaded metal path.

  • A complete voiced rig

    The pedal solves a diode-and-capacitor clipping circuit. Each amp has its own speaker/cabinet filter response, from open-combo character to closed-stack bite and sealed metal weight. A soft-knee compressor, darkening delay repeats and a diffused stereo room complete the rig.

In your setup

Start dry. Bring in the rig.

Start with Factory Default for a dry Drop-E guitar, Mute / Dead DI for tight rhythm detail, Drop-E Metal for heavy riffs or Blues Rock Lead for a warm lead. New instances start with FX off; turn it on to hear the selected rig. Factory rigs keep your current FX switch and playing-style choices, so changing a sound preserves the way you are playing.
