Modelled electric guitar · Reason Rack Extension (in development)

Electry

A modelled six-string electric guitar, a Stratocaster with a floating tremolo or a Telecaster, picked and fretted by two modelled hands from your keyboard. Fifteen keyswitches add bends, slides, harmonics and mutes, and the direct signal goes to the amp you choose.

  • Three single-coil pickups
  • 15 keyswitches
  • Strat & Telecaster

The guitar

Six strings, magnetic pickups and wiring physics.

Strings, bridge and pickups are modelled rather than sampled, and Volume, Neck Tone and Mid Tone work through a model of the guitar's own wiring, its cable and the amplifier input it feeds.

  • Strat or Tele, five-way or three-way

    On the Strat the five-way switch picks the bridge, middle or neck coil or one of the two pairs between; on the Tele a three-way switch picks bridge, neck or both. The selected coils light up on the panel.

  • Pick position, attack & feel

    Position moves the pick 25 to 250 mm from the bridge, Pick goes from thin to heavy, Strum spreads a chord over up to 40 ms, Release sets the fingers' lift noise and Feel adds a player's small variations.

  • 15 keyswitches & string solo

    Keys C0 to D1 switch dead notes, legato, slides, slide-ins, harmonics, pinch harmonics, whole and half bends, vibrato, stroke direction, fingers and damp. C-1 to F-1 solo strings. Every key is on the front, lit while active and ready to click.

  • Wheels, aftertouch & pedal

    The pitch wheel bends the last fretted string up to two semitones or, set to Arm, moves the tremolo arm; the mod wheel palm-mutes, aftertouch adds vibrato and the sustain pedal lets notes ring.

In the rack

Direct guitar signal for your amp rig.

Electry has no amp or effects of its own: its direct signal leaves on left and right, both carrying the same mono guitar, for Reason's amps or your pedals. The back also holds Note and Gate CV, Fretting Lookahead (off or 50 ms, compensated by Reason) and the pitch wheel's Arm or Finger mode, under a drawing of the modelled wiring.

Reason Rack Extension

Seven patches across both guitars.

Init and Bridge Twang, Glassy Middle, In Between, Neck Blues, Quack Rhythm, Slow Strum and Tele Twang, each set to sit at one level before your amp.

Rack Extension version history

Electry version history.

In development for Reason; the latest source is 2.11.2d1 (25 November 2026), and the last build 2.10.0f1. Since 2.6.0 it has gained a clickable keyswitch deck, playable keys from A0 to C8, a Telecaster beside the Strat, strums that ring on, measured pickups and one level across its patches. The panels shown are from 2.10.0f1, the last build with new panels.

2.11.2d1 Development update · 25 November 2026 · source only

With Fingers latched, a chord is plucked from its lowest string up whatever stroke was latched before.

2.11.1d1 Development update · 25 November 2026 · source only

Every patch plays at about −11 VU, and the output limiter now starts at −0.5 dBFS, out of the way of normal playing.

2.11.0d1 Development update · 25 November 2026 · source only

Measured Strat pickups with more resonance from 4 to 7 kHz, and the Telecaster’s own bridge and neck pickups. Every patch is set to one level.

2.10.0f1 Release candidate · 24 November 2026 · built

A harder strum is a faster strum. Builds 2.7.0d1 to 2.10.0d1, gathered here, add a Telecaster beside the Strat with its 3-way switch and fixed bridge, chord changes on the right strings, strums that ring on into the next chord, and a peak limiter.

2.6.2f1 Release candidate · 23 November 2026

Strat model fixes for rubs, slides, harmonics and pull-offs, lower CPU use, and the Clear key retired now that string solos last only while held.

2.6.0f1 Release candidate · 22 November 2026

A keyswitch deck that uses every piano key, a master Tune, saddle lamps that light while a string is soloed, and the pitch wheel bending a string by default.

2.5.1d2 Development build · 19 November 2026 · built

Adds front string-solo indicator lamps captioned C-1 through F-1 past the nut (with F#-1 clearing held solos) building directly on 2.5.1d1 string-solo voicing.

2.5.0f1 Development milestones · November 2026

Introduced the 8U fretboard panel, five-way pickup selector, 15 keyswitched articulations and modeled passive tone stack.
