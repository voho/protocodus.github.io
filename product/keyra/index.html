The piano

Five pianos, modelled.

Choose a concert, studio, parlor or baby grand or an upright, then voice its hammers and body from the front.

  • Hammers that answer your touch

    The hammer’s felt stiffens under a harder blow, so playing harder opens the tone and not just the volume. Hammer goes from soft, worn felt to hard, Dynamics sets how far apart your softest and hardest notes sit, and Felt lowers a practice felt.

  • Three pedals you can see

    Sustain lifts the dampers gradually, so half-pedal gives a shortened ring; sostenuto holds only the notes down when you press it; soft shifts a grand’s hammers off one string of each note. Hold C0, C♯0 or D0 on a keyboard without pedals.

  • Strings that ring together

    Strings whose dampers are lifted answer what you play, and the short lengths beyond the bridge ring with them. The modulation wheel lays felt on the strings, even on held keys.

  • Four microphone pairs

    Close over the hammers, XY at the lid, Live in the room and Distant across it each have their own level. Room sets the hall they hear, Lid opens or closes the piano, and Width narrows the image to mono.

In the rack

Strings and setup on the back.

The front keeps the piano, hammers, body, pedals and microphones in reach. On the back, choose the piano, age the strings, spread the unisons from pure to a bar piano, and set the tuner’s stretch, the duplex scales, Tuning from 400 to 480 Hz and the level of the action’s noises.

Note and Gate CV inputs bring rack sequencers into the same piano. Save the complete voicing as a Reason patch.

Reason Rack Extension

Twelve patches to start from.

Twelve factory patches, from the concert grand as built to a bar piano, set the piano and where you hear it from. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.

Rack Extension version history

Keyra development history.

Unreleased: no version of Keyra is in the Reason Shop yet. The latest version is 2.13.1f1.

2.13.1f1 Unreleased · Release candidate · 13 November 2026 · built, not yet run in Reason

The candidate for a first release. Each microphone pair’s image now follows the sound field, so the Close pair is wider and its bass holds together. The bass is tuned as a tuner tunes it: a grand’s lowest octaves on higher partials, the upright’s as a recorded upright reads. Built the way Reason builds it, the engine does the same work in under half the time on a development machine (Reason’s own meter is not read yet), a new device is ready sooner, and three controls left over from 1.0 that did nothing are gone.

2.11.0f1 Unreleased · Release candidate · 12 November 2026 · not yet built

Keyra becomes a physical model. In place of the measured pianos and the Build deck it plays five designed pianos, a concert grand, a studio grand, a parlor grand, a baby grand and an upright, with controls for the hammers, the strings, the body and four microphone pairs, on a new panel with twelve new factory patches. Songs made with 1.0 open on the concert grand and do not sound as they did.

1.0.0f2 Unreleased · Release candidate · 10 November 2026

The first release numbering of Keyra’s Rack Extension. The sound and levels are those of 0.4.38. This build fixes the Random Piano dice, which stopped the device in Reason in 1.0.0f1.

0.4.25d1 Unreleased · Development build · 6 November 2026

Phantom partials, the upright’s pitch glide, the action’s click above the body, beating undamped strings and the struck key ringing its own undamped string, with lower CPU for the same sound.

0.4.14d1 Unreleased · Rack Extension candidate · 3 November 2026

Each note in a chord now keeps its own level trim. The upright and two grands have revised output trims, and sustained notes use less processing in the piano engine.

0.4.13d1 Unreleased · Development build · 3 November 2026

Improves tonal balance and spectral continuity across all 88 keys, sympathetic resonance, repeated strikes and action mechanics on all six pianos. Keeps the Royal Conservatory panel finish and the existing factory voicings.

Reason Shop publication is separate from this development build.
