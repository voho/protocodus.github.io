The instrument

Strings, hammers, soundboard and sympathetic air.

Felt hammers strike the strings, the strings of each note share a bridge, and a soundboard carries the sound to the microphones, so a harder blow opens the tone, not only the volume.

  • Four grands & an upright

    Concert, studio, parlor and baby grands and an upright. Age the strings, spread each note’s unison from pure to a bar piano, and set the tuner’s Stretch.

  • Five microphones

    A close pair over the hammers, an XY pair at the lid, a pair in the room, a wide Distant pair and a Tube microphone over the strings, each with its own level, polarity switch and output; Room runs from a small room to a concert hall.

  • Three pedals, half-pedalling

    Sustain, sostenuto and soft pedals each have an On switch and an amount, so half-pedalling shortens the ring; C0, C♯0 and D0 stand in on a keyboard without pedals, and the mod wheel and Mute lay felt on sounding strings.

  • Voice it from the front

    Hammer, Dynamics, a practice Felt, Lid, Soundboard, Sympathetic strings and Duplex; keys land and dampers fall audibly, as loud as Mechanics sets.

In the rack

Main stereo mix plus dedicated microphone outs.

The rear panel carries the main stereo pair, a stereo pair each for the Close, XY, Live and Distant microphones and a mono Tube output, with Note and Gate CV and Tuning from 400 to 480 Hz.

Reason Rack Extension

Thirteen patches across five instruments.

Audition the factory patches in Reason’s browser or play them online with a mouse, computer keyboard or MIDI keyboard.

Rack Extension version history

Keyra release history.

Version 2.24.0, released on 24 November 2026, is the current Rack Extension in the Reason Shop. The latest development build is 2.24.4f1, which lowers DSP load on sustained chords and multi-mic routing.

2.24.4f1 Release candidate · 20 November 2026 · built

Cuts CPU cost on sustained chords and simultaneous mic outputs, restores pitch cleanly if reset mid-bend and matches macOS/Windows seeded string dispersion.

2.24.1d1 – 2.24.3f1 Development updates · 19–20 November 2026

Unified arrival/sustain calibration per microphone (including upper Concert Grand Distant and Upright C7), added single-capsule Tube direct/reflected path and retained duplex string motion under dampers.

2.24.0 Reason Shop release · 24 November 2026 · device 2.24.0f3

The first Reason Shop release: four grands and an upright, five microphones, all three pedals and 13 factory patches.
