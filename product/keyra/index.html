The piano

Combine six piano models.

Choose a grand or upright patch, then combine strings, hammers and body components in the rear Build deck.

  • Three pedals you can see

    Sustain, sostenuto and soft are on the panel with continuous travel. Sustain supports intermediate positions, sostenuto holds the notes present when it engages, and the soft pedal changes the next strike.

  • Silent pedal keys

    Hold C0, C♯0 or D0 for a momentary pedal gesture without leaving the keyboard.

  • A hand on the strings

    The modulation wheel gives a smooth mute that damps ringing strings, including notes still held on the keyboard.

  • The action and the microphone

    Mechanics sets the level of the action’s noises. Tube Mic moves from the stereo pair to a vintage tube, and brightness, sympathetic resonance and stereo width shape the rest.

In the rack

Build deck on the back.

The four-unit panel in Karelian birch burl, Crema Marfil marble and mammoth ivory keeps the pedals and the voicing in reach. The rear holds the Build deck that mixes the six pianos part by part.

Note and Gate CV inputs bring rack sequencers into the same piano. Save the complete voicing as a Reason patch.

Reason Rack Extension

Fourteen patches to start from.

Fourteen factory patches range from a concert grand to a felt upright and a close mono piano. The bank is in development and will be confirmed at release.

Rack Extension version history

Keyra development history.

The latest Rack Extension work in progress. Availability will be confirmed at release.

0.4.14d1 Rack Extension candidate · 3 November 2026

Each note in a chord now keeps its own level trim. The upright and two grands have revised output trims, and sustained notes use less processing in the piano engine.

0.4.13d1 Development build · 3 November 2026

Improves tonal balance and spectral continuity across all 88 keys, sympathetic resonance, repeated strikes and action mechanics on all six pianos. Keeps the Royal Conservatory panel finish and the existing factory voicings.

Reason Shop publication is separate from this development build.
