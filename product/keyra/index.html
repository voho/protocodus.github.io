The instrument

Strings, hammers, soundboard and sympathetic air.

Every velocity reshapes hammer compression, duplex overtone shimmer and un-damped string coupling continuously with no velocity layers or sample loops.

  • Four grands & an upright

    Move from Concert and Warm classical grands to Jazz/Stage models or an intimate Felt/Studio upright with distinct string scaling and cast-plate weight.

  • Five calibrated microphones

    Switch or blend Inside, Close, Player, Tube and Distant perspectives, each voiced from physical plate radiation across the soundboard.

  • Three pedals & duplex glow

    Half-pedalled Damper sympathy, Sostenuto selective catching and Una Corda shift act directly on the modelled strings and hammer strike.

  • Hammer, lid & age controls

    Open the lid continuously, soften or harden hammer felt, and add tuned piano age or subtle room decay.

In the rack

Main stereo mix plus dedicated microphone outs.

Tap independent stereo outputs for all five microphones on the 5U rear panel alongside Pitch, Damper and Sostenuto/Expression CV inputs.

Reason Rack Extension

Thirteen patches across five instruments.

Audition the factory patches in Reason’s browser or play them online with a mouse, computer keyboard or MIDI keyboard.
