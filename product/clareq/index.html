The equalizer

Less harshness. More room for the music.

Clareq adjusts 96 bands of minimum-phase EQ as the music plays. You set how far it goes, how fast it moves and where it may work, and you can listen to exactly what it takes away.

  • Tame resonances

    Anti-resonance reduces peaks that stand out from the spectrum around them, with transient protection designed to keep the attack. No AUX connection is needed.

  • Give other tracks room

    Connect audio to AUX 1–4 and Clareq makes complementary cuts in the main signal. Let a kick open space in a bass. Each reference has its own weight and is never mixed into the output.

  • Shape the response

    Strength and Max Cut set the reduction, Attack and Release its timing, and Sharpness the focus of the cuts. HP and LP limits keep the extremes of your audio untouched.

  • Digital or Analog

    Digital is clean, for subtle correction. Analog adds nonlinear character, and selectable oversampling suits it especially.

  • Across the stereo image

    Work in linked Stereo, independent Mid/Side, Mid only or Side only, then blend the result with Mix.

In the rack

See and hear your decisions.

The seven-unit panel draws the input, output, removed signal and AUX spectra. Switch on DIFF Listen to hear what Clareq takes away, then return to the mix and judge the result in context.

Connect the main input and up to four AUX references on the rear. Processing latency is about 32 ms. In Reason Rack Plugin one external stereo sidechain is available; further references can come from devices inside the Rack.

Reason Rack Extension

Start with Anti-resonance.

Insert Clareq on a harsh track, raise Anti-resonance (Self Weight) from zero, then adjust Strength until the edge goes. Connect an AUX when you want it to make room for another part.
