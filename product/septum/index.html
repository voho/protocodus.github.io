Septum

A ten-voice virtual-analog synthesizer modelled on a 2006 keyboard of the supersaw family, with two tones, nine waveforms, a multimode filter, arpeggiator and effects.

  • Two tones
  • 32 presets

The synth

An independent model, with no samples.

Septum is an original implementation with no firmware, ROM data, samples, captured audio or factory patch data. It is calibrated by ear and by measurement against named recordings of the original instrument.

  • Two tones

    Upper and Lower tones play Single, Dual or Split, each with two oscillators, nine waveforms including the supersaw, and Mix, Sync or Ring.

  • Filter and envelopes

    A multimode filter, three envelopes and two tempo-synced LFOs, with overdrive and an analog output stage.

  • Arpeggiator and effects

    An arpeggiator, delay into reverb with sixteen templates, and an external input with its own filter.

  • Every host

    VST3, CLAP and standalone on macOS, Windows and Linux, plus Audio Unit on macOS.

Plug-in

Thirty-two presets.

Septum’s own bank of 32 presets covers leads, basses, pads, bells, splits and sequences. No Rack Extension edition exists yet.
