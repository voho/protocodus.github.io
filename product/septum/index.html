Model foundation

More than a stack of saws.

These are the synthesis principles behind Septum’s development. The Rack edition’s feature set is still being shaped.

  • Detune with a defined shape

    Seven sawtooth oscillators retain separate phases and use unequal pitch offsets around a central oscillator. A nonlinear spread curve changes the beating pattern as detune rises, turning a close chorus into a broad moving sound.

  • Interaction between oscillators

    Synchronization resets oscillator phase within the waveform cycle. Ring modulation multiplies the signals, while a saturating feedback path feeds delayed energy into the oscillator. Each process creates a different family of moving partials.

  • Filtering and nonlinear color

    The model foundation treats resonant filtering, drive and output response as successive parts of the signal path. Saturation uses antialiasing to control unwanted high-frequency foldback as the sound is driven harder.
