Virtual-analog polysynth

Septum

A ten-voice virtual-analog synthesizer that models the Roland SH-201 from 2006, the last keyboard of the calculated-supersaw family that began with the JP-8000.

Inside the synth

Two complete tones
in every patch.

UPPER and LOWER each run two oscillators into a mix and modulation stage, a multimode filter and an amp, with three envelopes and two LFOs apiece.

01

Seven-saw SUPER SAW

SUPER SAW puts seven sawtooth waves inside one oscillator slot, with the PW knob setting the pitch spread. The detune offsets and the spread curve follow Adam Szabo's published JP-8000 measurements, the seven phases are randomised and free-running at each note-on, and the summed stack passes a high-pass tracked to the note's own fundamental.

02

Two tones per patch

One patch holds two complete tones, UPPER and LOWER, each with its own oscillators, filter, amp, three envelopes and two LFOs, plus per-tone sends into the shared delay and reverb. SINGLE, DUAL and SPLIT keyboard modes give ten voices, halved to five and five in DUAL.

03

Filter that self-oscillates

Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass and bypass at -12 or -24 dB per octave, with cutoff and resonance each spanning 0 to 127 and key follow from -200 to +200 in steps of 10. Resonance turned far right reaches sustained self-oscillation, exactly as the manual warns, held bounded by a soft-knee limiter.

04

Arpeggiator and rear input

The arpeggiator implements the documented 32 by 16 style grid, with shuffled divisions, tie chains, all twelve motifs, octave range, accent, velocity, end step, hold and split arpeggio. The 16 styles supplied are original patterns rather than Roland data. The rear INPUT jacks add input volume, centre cancel and a four-type AUDIO FILTER, and EXT-IN is selectable as an oscillator waveform.

What it does not do

Stated plainly.

The D Beam, the step recorder, tap tempo and CLOCK SOURCE with external MIDI clock are not included. The recorder, SysEx transmit and the USB audio topology are deferred, the codec's own digital filter is left to the host's converters, and a patch name is dropped on load.

Septum is an independent original implementation, not affiliated with or licensed by Roland Corporation. It contains no firmware, ROM data, samples, captured audio, factory patches or factory arpeggio styles.
