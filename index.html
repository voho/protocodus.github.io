Physically modelled acoustic guitar

Acustra

Six vibrating strings exchange energy through the bridge and guitar body. Pick contact, string stiffness, frequency-dependent damping and a modelled piezo circuit connect the attack, resonance and capture into one playable instrument.

Six strings · Four body shapes · Microphone & piezo
Shop release: 1.9.0

Circuit-modelled polysynth

YouKnow

Persistent voice cards, a nonlinear four-stage filter and a clocked bucket-brigade chorus recreate the interactions behind the Juno-106’s sound. Oscillator history, component variation and signal-dependent saturation give each phrase its own character.

1–16 voices · 100 authored Rack patches · Eight CV inputs
Shop release: 1.3.5

Explore YouKnow →
Physically modelled mallet percussion

Maremba

Carved bars, plucked steel tines and nonlinear mallet contact meet resonating tubes, boxes and gourds. Play marimba, balafon or kalimba, with sympathetic vibration, pressure-driven buzz and three independent capture paths.

Four instruments · 20 patches · Seven audio outputs
Shop release: 1.5.7

Creative codec effect

Lamer Degrader

Real MP3 and Ogg Vorbis encode/decode cycles expose the texture of lossy compression. Spectral bit allocation and quantization respond to the source; repeated generations deepen transient smearing, metallic edges and disappearing detail.

Two codecs · 8–256 kbps targets · 1–4 passes
Shop release: 1.0.0
