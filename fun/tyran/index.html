      Flight manual

      Born to fly.

      Break the swarm, survive each sector’s capital ship and refit between worlds.

      Vanguard / Flight controls

      w a s d Move
      space Primary gun
      q Plasma mortar
      e Nova charge

      Meet the swarm. Squadrons fly in along looping paths and settle into a breathing hive at the top of the sky. Shoot them as they arrive. From the hive they peel off and dive at you; a ship shot down mid-dive is worth double.

      Wipe out squadrons. Destroy every ship in a squadron before any escapes for a bonus. Every third squadron you wipe drops a power core.

      Power up. Power cores widen your primary gun through five levels. A hit that gets past your shield knocks one core loose; catch it before it drifts away.

      Fly with drones. Wing drones copy your primary fire and soak up stray rounds. Heavy cruisers drop them; the shop sells them.

      Beware the captor. Its tractor beam steals a wing drone, which then fires at you. Shoot the captor down to rescue it. With no drone to steal, the beam drains shields and energy.

      Read the lancers. A lancer paints a thin firing line before its beam. Leave the line.

      Detonate a nova. e clears every hostile round, strikes every ship on screen and shields you briefly. You launch each sector with at least two.

      Keep flying. Reserve ships continue the sector after your ship is lost, at the cost of two power levels and a drone. Score milestones award extra ships.

      Choose your fire. Plasma Mortar fires gently guided blasts toward large nearby enemies ahead, spending 20 of your 100 energy per shot. When empty, it waits for 40 energy; your primary keeps firing.

      Fire or recover. Firing leaves only a trickle of shield recovery: 1 shield per second before upgrades. Release both guns for 0.75 seconds to recover at 12.5 per second. You still need to dodge until the normal recharge delay after a hit has passed. The shield arrow shows faster or slower recovery.

      Break barriers. Shielded gunships, aces, veteran elites and later cruisers carry a recharging barrier. Pulse and plasma strip it fastest, scatter pellets glance off and Lance needles push part of each hit straight through. Your own shield stops energy rounds, but rams, meteors, mines and bombs push some damage through it. When it collapses it vents a pulse that clears nearby rounds, then needs a moment longer to reboot.

      Fly the mission. Every sector sets two objectives, shown when you pause and in the shop before launch. Goals such as wiping squadrons pay the moment you meet them; accuracy, an untouched hull or losing no ships are judged when the sector is cleared. Objectives, accuracy and losses grade the sector from S to D, and better grades add to the clear bonus. Halfway through a sector you pass a checkpoint: if your last ship falls, you can retry from there.

      Chain the sky. A double kill, multi kill or rampage briefly boosts damage and blast radius. Take a hit or let the timer expire and the overcharge fades.

      Read the guardian. Its armor seals between attack cycles. When the core opens, glowing weak points take bonus damage.

      Challenging stages. After odd-numbered sectors, forty ships fly an acrobatic pass without firing back. Hit every one for a perfect bonus.

      Watch the ground. Destroy green-marked supply buildings to release repair, credits or temporary bonuses. Trees, rocks and vegetation stay intact.

      Build your ship. Between sectors, buy the Scatter Cannon or Lance Driver, wing drones, nova charges, reserve ships and permanent upgrades. Fire rate and Fire power each offer six small 2% steps. After the tenth environment, keep your equipment and begin a new cycle with stronger enemies and richer rewards. The campaign keeps going.

      Pick up where you left off. Your campaign saves automatically. Sector practice leaves your campaign untouched. Esc / p pauses; v toggles sound. On the title screen ↑ ↓ move the highlight, ← → change the sector or difficulty, enter confirms, c resumes, f practices the selected sector and h opens this manual.

      On touch screens. Drag the left flight control to steer; hold Pulse or Plasma on the right to fire, tap Nova to detonate.
