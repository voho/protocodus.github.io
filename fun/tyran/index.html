Flying solo. Either WASD or the arrow keys steer your ship. Use either Control key, Space or Enter to fire.

Choose your edge. Press 1–6 to switch fire profiles: pulse, scatter, lance, seeker, plasma or arc.

Stay in motion. Dodge enemy fire and use the whole flight lane.

Chain the sky. A double kill, multi kill or rampage briefly boosts damage and blast radius. Take a hit or let the timer expire and the overcharge fades.

Read the guardian. Its armor seals between attack cycles. When the core opens, glowing weak points take bonus damage.

Leave an impression. Enemy structures and scenery can be destroyed. Fly through dropped credits to collect them.

Build your ship. Upgrade weapons, hull, shields and shield recharge in the service bay.

Take a break. Press ESC / P or the pause button during flight. M toggles sound.

On touch screens. Drag the left flight control to steer and hold the right fire control.
