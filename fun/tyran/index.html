    Flight manual

    Born to fly.

    Destroy the enemy fleet and survive each sector’s capital ship. Collect the credits left in your wake and refit between worlds.

    Fly with the mouse. Move the pointer across the arena to guide player one and hold the left button to fire. Movement keys take control until you move the mouse again. Player two keeps the arrow keys.

    Flying solo. Either WASD or the arrow keys steer your ship. Use either Control key, Space or Enter to fire.

    Choose your edge. Pulse Array fires fast, precise twin bolts. Plasma Mortar trades speed for a heavy blast that catches nearby targets. Press L Alt / Option for player one or R Alt / Option for player two. Each pilot switches independently; both weapons benefit from armament upgrades.

    Stay in motion. Dodge enemy fire and use the whole flight lane.

    Chain the sky. A double kill, multi kill or rampage briefly boosts damage and blast radius. Take a hit or let the timer expire and the overcharge fades.

    Read the guardian. Its armor seals between attack cycles. When the core opens, glowing weak points take bonus damage.

    Leave an impression. Enemy structures and scenery can be destroyed. Fly through dropped credits to collect them.

    Watch the ground. Turrets charge an amber warning before firing. Destroy green-marked supply structures to release repair, credits or temporary bonuses.

    Catch a power-up. The lightning pickup gives 65% faster firing for 10 seconds. The shield pickup makes you invulnerable for 10 seconds. Only the collecting pilot gets the bonus; another pickup refreshes its timer.

    Build your ship. Start a new campaign in sector one. Clear each world to reach the shop, then spend credits on weapons, hull, shields and shield recharge. Both co-op ships share these upgrades.

    Pick up where you left off. Your campaign saves automatically, including your sector, credits, score and equipment. Choose Resume campaign to return to your flight or the shop. New campaign starts over. Sector practice leaves your campaign untouched.

    Take a break. Press ESC / P or the pause button during flight. M toggles sound.

    On touch screens. Drag the left flight control to steer and hold the right fire control. Tap your weapon name to switch.
