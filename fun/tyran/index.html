      Tyran. Flight manual

      Flight manual

      Clear the waves, defeat the guardian, upgrade your ship.

      Controls

      Touch. Drag left to steer. Hold Pulse or Plasma to fire; tap Nova to detonate.

      Menus and upgrades. Use arrows or WASD to navigate, Enter or Space to select. On the title screen, left/right or A/D changes the sector or difficulty. Tab also moves between controls.

      Manual. Up/down or W/S scrolls. Enter, Space or Escape returns. Shortcuts: c resume, f practice, h manual.

      Enemy fleet

      Swarms. Shoot ships on arrival or as they dive. Dive kills score double. Clear a whole squadron before any escape for a bonus; every third wipe drops a power core.

      Captors. Tractor beams steal drones. Destroy the captor to rescue yours. Without a drone, the beam drains shields and energy.

      Lancers. Move away from the warning line before the beam fires.

      Guardians. Wait for the armored core to open, then target glowing weak points for extra damage.

      Challenge stages. After odd-numbered sectors, hit all forty unarmed ships for a perfect bonus.

      Weapons and shields

      Plasma. Guided blasts cost 20 energy. After depletion, plasma waits for 40 energy; your primary keeps firing.

      Power cores. Upgrade your primary through five levels. Hull damage knocks a core loose; catch it to recover.

      Drones. Copy your primary fire and absorb stray rounds. Buy them in the hangar or collect them from heavy cruisers.

      Nova. Press e to clear hostile rounds, damage every visible enemy and briefly shield your ship. Each sector starts with at least two charges.

      Shield recovery. Release both guns for 0.75 seconds to recharge faster: 12.5 shield per second, versus 1 while firing, before upgrades. Recovery still waits for the delay after a hit. The shield arrow indicates the rate.

      Enemy barriers. Pulse and plasma strip them quickly; scatter is weaker, while Lance bypasses part of the barrier. Your shield blocks energy, but collisions, mines and bombs can damage hull through it. A collapsed shield clears nearby rounds and briefly reboots.

      Grazes and chains. Near misses restore energy and earn score. Chained kills briefly boost damage and blast radius; taking a hit ends the boost.

      Campaign

      Objectives. Two per sector, listed in Pause and the hangar. Completed goals pay credits. Accuracy, hull and ship-loss goals are judged at sector end; your S–D rank adds to the clear bonus.

      Checkpoints and ships. A mid-sector checkpoint allows a retry after defeat. Reserve ships keep you flying, at the cost of two power levels and a drone. Score milestones award extra ships.

      Supplies. Destroy green-marked buildings for repairs, credits or bonuses. Trees, rocks and vegetation stay intact.

      Hangar. Buy weapons, drones, nova charges, reserve ships and permanent upgrades. Fire rate and Fire power each offer six 2% steps. After ten worlds, keep your equipment and enter a harder cycle with greater rewards.

      Saves. Campaigns autosave in this browser. Practice keeps your campaign save intact.
