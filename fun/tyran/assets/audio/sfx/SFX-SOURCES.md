# Tyran sound effects

Downloaded on 2026-09-26 from Kenney's official asset pages. Both pages explicitly identify their audio as **Creative Commons CC0** (public-domain dedication); the original archive license files are preserved alongside this document. Attribution is optional. Thank you, Kenney.

- **Sci-fi Sounds 1.0**, by Kenney: [source and license](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), [original archive](https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/sci-fi-sounds/6b296f9ecf-1677589334/kenney_sci-fi-sounds.zip). Archive notice: `KENNEY-SCI-FI-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Digital Audio**, by Kenney Vleugels: [source and license](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio), [original archive](https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/digital-audio/216eac4753-1677590265/kenney_digital-audio.zip). Archive notice: `KENNEY-DIGITAL-LICENSE.txt`.
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Only the following 18 effects are shipped. Source Ogg Vorbis files were decoded to mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV for broad browser support, with metadata removed. The game applies volume envelopes, slight playback-rate variation, and a short tail limit to rapid weapon sounds. No other creative modifications were made. File checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
