# Mission loading artwork

These four original images were generated for Hedgerow on 6 November 2026.
They are cinematic reconstructions of the game's fictional missions, not
archival photographs or evidence of actual events. No third-party photograph
was incorporated into the shipped images.

- `saint-clair.webp`: a rainy Norman sunken lane and damaged limestone village
  at dusk, July 1944.
- `la-varenne.webp`: a cratered meadow, orchard and hedgerow gun battery in the
  early morning of 6 June 1944.
- `pont-tournant.webp`: a night glider landing beside a canal swing bridge,
  6 June 1944.
- `prague.webp`: a fictional Vinohrady street during the Prague uprising,
  with a prewar tram barricade and Czech resistance fighters, 7 May 1945.

Each is a 1672 × 941 WebP. The compositions leave space on the left for the
loading menu. The same local files illustrate the mission selector, so no
external image service is needed at runtime.
