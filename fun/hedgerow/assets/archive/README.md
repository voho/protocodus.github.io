# Historical photographs

These are real photographs from the Normandy campaign, used as historical
reference in the menu and loading experience. They do not depict Hedgerow's
fictional mission locations. The credits link to collection or rights records.

All three originals were made by U.S. military personnel in their official
duties and are public domain in the United States. The image-specific rights
records are linked in `provenance.json`. A repository containing a copy is only
a download mirror, not the source of the rights determination.

The supplied copies retain the complete photographic frame and original black
and white appearance. They were resized proportionally to 1600 pixels wide and
encoded as WebP (Pillow, quality 86, method 6). No invented scene content,
colorization, or AI reconstruction was added. Existing Commons restorations are
identified in the provenance record. The UI may crop the presentation to fit its
frame; the local image files themselves are not cropped.

`provenance.json` records immutable download URLs, source and output SHA-256
checksums, and the rights basis checked on 6 November 2026. All three images
together are under 1 MB, and the page only requests its selected photograph.
