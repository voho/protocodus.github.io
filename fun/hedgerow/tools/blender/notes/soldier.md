# Soldier set — notes

Built by `tools/blender/build_soldier.py` (helpers in `soldier_parts.py`); this file is
regenerated by every full build. All positions below are in **glTF / three.js space** (Y up,
the soldier faces **+Z**, his left is **+X**) unless marked Blender.

    /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
        --python fun/hedgerow/tools/blender/build_soldier.py [-- soldier|weapons] [nopreview]

### v4 (realism pass: face and body; the face revised in v4.1 below)

* **Head**: one surface from the crown to inside the collar (head and neck merged, no seam under the jaw),
  generated on a fine grid of horizontal superellipse rings (`HEAD4`) with the anatomy as displacement
  (`face4_disp`): brow ridge over deep-set eyes, orbits, nose with bridge, tip lobule, alar wings and
  nostrils (`nose4`), cheekbones and zygomatic arches with the hollow below, nasolabial folds, philtrum,
  lips with Cupid's bow, vermilion and white roll, stomion and commissures (`lips4`), mentolabial sulcus and
  chin pad, jaw angle and mandible border, masseters, temples, Adam's apple, sternocleidomastoids, nape,
  a little hair volume above the hairline. Eyelids are wrapped on the eyeballs (`lid4`: tucked fissure,
  rounded margins, pretarsal lid, crease and fold, lower-lid roll, lid–cheek groove, pink inner corner)
  and joined to the orbit with a smooth max. The grid (~100k quads) is decimated with a symmetric
  quadric collapse to ~6.2k tris. Neck ~37–38 cm round at the collar (was 34.6).
* **Eyes**: ellipsoidal globes (the lids rest on them to the canthi) with a corneal bulge and limbal
  sulcus; baked sclera with veins and pink corners, grey-green iris with fibres, hazel collarette and a dark
  limbal ring, black pupil; roughness 0.03–0.10 on the eye only. Apex unchanged (v3's `EYE_R`).
* **Ears**: sculpted helix rim, antihelix, scapha, concha, tragus, antitragus and lobe (~820 tris each).
* **Skin** (`r_skin4`): satin (roughness ~0.50–0.62, 0.50 on the T-zone, 0.47 lips, 0.64 stubble), warm
  zones (ruddy cheeks, nose, ears and chin, yellower forehead, darker sockets), blue-grey stubble on the
  jaw, chin and upper lip, clipped hair on the scalp, hair-strand eyebrows, lash lines, lips with a
  vermilion border; pores, crow's feet, forehead, under-eye, nasolabial, lip and neck lines in the
  normal map; mud smears, dirt in the creases, sweat on forehead and nose; dirtier hands.
* **Hands** (`build_hand4`): same grip frames; denser back of the hand with extensor tendons and
  knuckles, bony finger joints, nail plates (`nail`) on the fingers and thumb.
* **Body**: `TORSO_JACKET4` (squarer shoulders, rounder and deeper chest and back; the clips keep
  `TORSO_JACKET` for the rifle butt); sleeves carry the arm under the wool (`arm_muscles`: deltoid,
  biceps, triceps, bony elbow, forearm masses, flatter wrist); trousers the leg (`leg_muscles`:
  quadriceps, vastus medialis, hamstrings, inner thigh, kneecap, calf) on fuller thighs; calves in the
  leggings and jackboots. Collars widened for the thicker neck; the German stand collar dips at the front.
* **Cloth** (on top of the library wool/drill, `register_wear`): sweat stains with tide marks under the
  arms and down the back, ~3.5 mm diagonal twill wales in the normal map, besides v3's wet shoulders and
  legs, muddy knees, dusty elbows and mud splashed on the lower legs.
* **Skinning**: the sleeve cap over the shoulder blends into clavicle/chest, the jacket over the joint
  takes some upper arm, the elbow blend is wider (±5 cm); the head blends into the neck along a line from
  under the chin to the skull base (`w_headneck`).
* **Chin straps**: M1 straps run from the bales along the outside of the shell and buckle at the back,
  the tail hooked over the rim (as worn in Normandy); the German strap lies on the skin in front of the
  ears, over the jaw (buckle on the left) and under the chin, skinned like the skin under it; in v4.1 its
  vertices copy the morph of the skin under them (`add_face_morphs(follow=...)`), so every face keeps it on.
* **Clips**: not re-animated (bones, rest pose, clip names, frame counts, sockets and reload events are
  identical to v3); only the deaths' automatic grounding re-ran against the new body, which moves their
  `root` translation by ≤ 9 mm.

### v4.1 (second face pass: shorter lower face, fuller lips, wider jaw, distinct face shapes)

* **Shorter lower third**: the face is designed in a warped z (`head4_warp`, `WARP4`): between the nose
  base and under the chin it is built 16 % shorter than designed (smooth, identity above z_rel −0.038, a
  constant 12 mm lift below −0.130), so the eyes, nose root and skull do not move; the shader masks use the
  same warp (`_sg_warp`). The nose is 10 % shorter towards its root, the chin set higher and the under-chin
  tucked, the hairline raised 1.8 cm (under the helmet).
* **Wider-reading jaw**: narrower at the cheek and jaw levels in absolute terms (anatomical widths), but a
  squarer lower face (superellipse front exponent up to 2.25), a stronger jaw angle and masseter over a
  slimmer upper neck, a ~4 cm chin with mental tubercles, a lighter under-cheekbone hollow.
* **Fuller lips** (`lips4`): taller and fuller vermilion, defined Cupid's bow and philtrum columns, a soft
  parting line, depressed and darker corners with the modiolus beside them.
* **Eyes read better**: lighter socket tint and a softer occlusion profile for the skin (`skin4`: 0.55).
* **Ears** moved in 4.4 mm with the narrower head.
* German chin strap vs skin (true distance to the posed skin, all 17 clips every other frame, faces rest /

### v4.2 (distance LODs)

`allied_lod` and `axis_lod`: skinned mesh nodes, children of `rig`, bound to the same glTF skin as the full
meshes (same 20 joints and inverse bind matrices) and drawing with the same glTF material (`allied` / `axis`,
the same three images — nothing duplicated); COLOR_0 flat white, no morph targets. Made by `soldier_parts.make_lod`
from the final baked mesh, before skinning:
* parts smaller than 2.5 cm (buttons, studs, snaps, hobnails, hooks, nails) are dropped — sub-pixel at LOD
  distances; faces enclosed at depth in another shell (every ray from them meets a surface from behind, ≥ 6 mm
  away: eyeball backs, limb caps and the neck bottom inside the torso, hand roots in the cuffs) are dropped;
  faces merely covered by another layer are kept so the decimated covers never open holes;
* the rest is collapse-decimated to 5800 tris (silhouette kept: helmet, shoulders, webbing,
  pack, boots, arms and hands). The shared atlas has many small UV islands, so ~80 % of the LOD's vertices
  are split along island seams (~78 bytes per LOD triangle in the GLB): the two LODs add ~0.9 MB, soldier.glb is
  7.2 MB (over the 7 MB budget; accepted for v4.2);
* UVs: collapse decimation smears UVs across island seams, so every LOD face is re-projected onto ONE island
  of the full mesh's atlas (the island under its centre, on the same part; a face switches to the island its
  neighbours use when its corners lie on it too, for fewer seams) and its corners take that island's UV at
  their closest point — the baked atlas maps unchanged. Islands are not convex, so each face's UV triangle is
  then tested against a 1024² raster of its island; a face that would span empty atlas is shrunk round the texel
  under its centre until it lies on the island (orientation kept, so the tangent frame stays valid);
* normals: every LOD corner takes the full mesh's split normal at the closest point on a face of the same part
  facing the same way (custom normals). The decimated mesh's own smooth normals tilted where long triangles met
  a turned edge — the jackboot tops, where the shaft shared its top ring with the turned-in lip: the shaft
  caught the sun as white glints in the first v4.2 build;
* skin weights: from the full mesh at the nearest surface point on the same part facing the same way,
  barycentric, top 4, normalised.
* `allied_lod`: 5791 tris (full mesh 28347); 80 small parts and 912 enclosed faces dropped before decimating 25544 tris; 65 faces' UVs shrunk onto their island; parent `rig`, skin 0 (full 0), material [0] (full [0]), morph targets [0].
* `axis_lod`: 5799 tris (full mesh 28995); 80 small parts and 1563 enclosed faces dropped before decimating 25471 tris; 91 faces' UVs shrunk onto their island; parent `rig`, skin 0 (full 0), material [1] (full [1]), morph targets [0].
* Previews: `previews/soldier/{allied,axis}_lod_compare.png` — rows rest, aim, run, death_fwd; columns full 10 m,
  LOD 10 m, full 30 m, LOD 30 m (720 px, 60° vertical field of view, cropped and blown up pixel for pixel).
* `character.js` draws `<outfit>` near and `<outfit>_lod` far (switch at 30 m, back at 26 m; tests/lod-check.mjs);
  the LOD has no face shapes, so the per-soldier face only shows on the full mesh.

### Bones (rest pose)

Every bone: local **+Y runs along the bone** to its child (Blender convention, kept by the exporter:
a child whose head sits on its parent's tail has rest translation `(0, parent_length, 0)`). For `root`, `hips`, `spine`,
`chest`, `neck`, `head`, the arms and the legs the local **+Z points to the character's front** at rest
(three +Z) and **X = Y × Z**; for the feet +Z points up. So for `spine`/`chest` the local X axis is the
horizontal towards the character's left (three +X) and **a positive rotation about local X bends forward /
pitches the rifle down**; aim pitch up = negative angle about local X. (Legs point down, so their X
is three −X: + about X swings the leg back / bends the knee.)

Upper/lower split as in the brief: legs = `root`, `hips`, `thigh_*`, `shin_*`, `foot_*`; upper =
`spine` … `hand_*`. The upper-body overlays key `spine` relative to `hips`, so the overlay inherits the
locomotion clip's hip sway (walk ±4° yaw).

**Aim pitch**: apply it on `spine` about its local X: in `aim`/`crouch_aim` the spine has no yaw or roll
relative to the hips (the bladed shooter's twist, −30°, is all in `chest`), so spine-local X is the
horizontal axis perpendicular to the barrel and the muzzle stays on the centre line while pitching.
Pitching `chest` about its own local X would swing the muzzle sideways (chest is yawed).

### Weapon sockets

`socket_weapon` (child of `hand_r`): translation (-0.0353, 0.0680, -0.0037), rotation (x, y, z, w)
(0.1554, 0.5235, 0.7790, 0.3081), scale 1. A weapon from weapons.glb added as its child with an identity
transform has its grip in the right palm, barrel forward, top up — the same placement the clips were
authored with (verified: socket world matrix in the GLB equals the Blender one converted to Y-up,
error 3.6e-07). At rest the barrel points 44° off the forearm axis
(the wrist is cocked as on a rifle wrist/pistol grip).

`socket_weapon_l` (child of `hand_l`, extra): translation (0.0393, 0.0680, -0.0042), rotation
(-0.1593, -0.0860, -0.6889, 0.7019). It is the left-hand grip frame expressed in weapon axes: put a weapon under
it with **position = −offhand anchor** (in the weapon's own glTF space) and identity rotation and the
weapon's `offhand` point sits in the left palm. `throw` is authored with the rifle there (move the rifle
to this socket for the throw and put the grenade on `socket_weapon`: its origin is its centre, which then
sits in the right fist).

In every two-handed clip the left hand holds weapon-space point **(0, −0.215, −0.012) Blender =
(0, −0.012, 0.215) glTF** (the Garand's `offhand`); other weapons' `offhand` anchors differ by a few cm
(table below) — close enough, or the game can IK the left hand to the anchor.

## kit.py

* v3: the face/eye/hand texel boost now uses bake.py's new `scale_islands` hook (`soldier_parts.island_scaler`); nothing in bake.py is patched any more.
* `bake_model` ends with `kit.flat_color(obj, 1.0)`, so baked meshes still export a COLOR_0 of 1.0 (~4 bytes/vertex); harmless, but could be dropped if the shader no longer needs it.
* `export(objs, path, animations=True)` works for skins + actions; the soldier uses its own `export_scene.gltf` call only to turn on `export_optimize_animation_size` (+ `keep_anim_armature`).
* `reset()` leaves the scene at 24 fps: an animated set must set `scene.render.fps = 30` before writing actions, or every clip exports 25 % too long.
* `export()` logs the triangle count of the listed objects only, so exporting `[rig]` logs 0 tris.
* `preview()` hides any mesh whose parent is not in the list, e.g. a weapon parented to a socket empty unless it is passed explicitly; it frames from evaluated (posed) vertices while `bounds()` uses the rest mesh.
* The v1-reported issues are fixed in this kit (noise seeding, deterministic jitter, boolean use_self, uv_cylinder factor); none of them affected this set.
