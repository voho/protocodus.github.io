How to play

One line, every cell.

Every clue gets one patch.

For grown-upsDrag from the head of the line, or tap neighbouring cells. Retrace the line to erase, or use Undo. On a keyboard, ← ↑ → ↓ draw and ⌫ steps back. U undo · N new puzzle · R start again · H hint · M sound · T next world. The world changes by itself every couple of minutes. The stars pick the board size. Every board has exactly one solution. Hints highlight a route or a wrong turn; you draw or retrace the line yourself.
For grown-upsDrag between opposite corners, or tap one corner then the other. The preview shows its size and whether it fits. Tap a placed patch to lift it again; Escape cancels a selection.
