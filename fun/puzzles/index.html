How to play

One line, every cell.

Every clue gets one patch.

  • 1Start on 1, finish on the last number. The line passes the numbers in order.
  • Fill the board. Every cell is visited exactly once, moving between neighbouring cells.
  • Walls stop the line. It cannot cross a glowing wall, or itself.
  • Cover the whole board with rectangles. Each patch holds exactly one clue, and no two patches overlap.
  • 6A number is the patch's size, in cells.
  • The icon is its shape: a square, a wide bar (wider than tall) or a tall bar. Without a number, the neighbours decide its size.

Drag from the head of the line, or tap neighbouring cells. Retrace the line to erase, or use Undo. On a keyboard, ← ↑ → ↓ draw and ⌫ steps back.
