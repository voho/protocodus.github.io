How to play

Every clue gets one patch.

  • Cover the whole board with rectangles. Each patch holds exactly one clue, and no two patches overlap.
  • 6A number is the patch's size, in cells.
  • The icon is its shape: square, wide (wider than tall) or tall. The dashed icon takes any rectangle.

Drag from a clue to draw its patch; tap a patch to lift it again. On a keyboard, ← ↑ → ↓ move, Enter starts and places a patch and X lifts one. U undo · N new puzzle · R reset · H hint · M sound. Every board has exactly one solution. Hints highlight a patch to draw or reconsider; you place or lift it yourself.
