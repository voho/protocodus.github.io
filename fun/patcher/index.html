How to play

Every clue gets one patch.

  • Cover the whole board with rectangles. Each patch holds exactly one clue, and no two patches overlap.
  • 6A number is the patch's size, in cells.
  • The icon is its shape: a square, a wide bar (wider than tall) or a tall bar. Without a number, the neighbours decide its size.

Drag between opposite corners, or tap one corner then the other. The preview shows its size and whether it fits. Tap a placed patch to lift it again; Escape cancels a selection. On a keyboard, ← ↑ → ↓ move, Enter starts and places a patch and X lifts one. U undo · N new puzzle · R reset · H hint · M sound · T theme. Try a Daily puzzle, or New for an endless supply. Six themes bring their own scenery, colours and weather. Every board has exactly one solution. Hints highlight a patch to draw or reconsider; you place or lift it yourself.
