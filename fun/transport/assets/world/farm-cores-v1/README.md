# Farm building cores

Ten generated **2×2 building cores** serve current **5×5 farm plots**: grain farm, dairy farm, vegetable farm, orchard and livestock farm, each in Taiga and Desert. These are the two eligible farm climates. Barns, grain silos, milk tanks, packing shelters and broad greenhouses identify the kinds. The native terrain renderer supplies surrounding fields, tracks, paddocks and perimeter fences; those full plots are absent from the core sprites. Published recipe 9 retains its historic 7×7 farm geography and uses the same fixed-size cores.

The active version 4 family was generated on 7 October 2026 with `image_gen.imagegen` and the canonical `buildingGenerationPrompt()` in [`sprite-art-direction.js`](../../../sprite-art-direction.js). Each core represents a 32 metre square yard at the shared physical scale, with a maximum 20 metre architectural envelope. The expected orthographic ground axes are ±0.5, ordinary personnel openings are 2.1 metres high, storeys are 3 metres, and loading openings are 4.2 metres. Broad muted roof and wall masses replace the previous dense tree fringes and crisp ground diamonds. Quiet irregular farmyard grass or worn sand blends with the native fields.

Each climate retains its canonical `generation-job.json` and `generation-prompt.json`, exact final `generation-prompt-camera-v4.json`, untouched `source-camera-v4.png`, registration landmarks in `registration-camera-v4.json`, `source-camera-v4-registered.png`, `packing-v4.json` and `provenance-camera-v4.json`. The references include the shared semantic palette and mathematical ground registration guide, plus a farm-specific construction sheet generated by [`tools/generate-farm-core-reference.mjs`](../../../tools/generate-farm-core-reference.mjs) directly from the canonical metre projection. Taiga `source-camera-v4-reference.png` preserves the exact painted reference used for the Desert climate edit. Earlier source artwork and exact version 3 jobs/prompts remain archived.

[`tools/repack-farm-cores-registered.py`](../../../tools/repack-farm-cores-registered.py) applies **one shared physical scale across all ten sprites**, based on the joint 36.4-source-pixel mean of measured personnel-opening frames. The uniform source-to-256 factor is 0.307692. Actual door heights after packing range from 9.85 to 12.92 pixels around the 11.2 pixel target; these measured values are exposed per cell rather than inferred from the prompt. The painted source axes also remain approximate: measured absolute edge slopes range from 0.471 to 0.549 in Taiga and 0.500 to 0.532 in Desert. The source is neither sheared nor individually fitted to conceal that variation.

The actual farmyard ground centre is registered to **(128,192) in the 256 pixel master**, independent of alpha bounds, shadow tips and roof height. Visible opposite barn-floor corners and the known barn centre in metres recover that point; covered front corners and hidden rear corners are explicitly marked inferred. Integer translation leaves at most 0.206 master pixels of recorded registration error. `atlas.json` exposes the actual source coordinates, corner visibility, camera line measurements, doorway head/sill endpoints, uniform factor, resulting physical dimensions and complete normalized bounds under `physicalCalibration.cells`.

The packer preserves complete RGBA cutouts in fixed 512 pixel registered cells, then uses `--aligned --preserve-grid-scale --no-sharpen --max-cell 256` to produce the 256 pixel master and 16, 32, 64 and 128 pixel display cells. `atlas-512.png` exports the calibrated source cells directly for dense Town and Detail displays; it never enlarges a thumbnail. All six cells preserve the same physical grid. The sixth slot remains transparent at every density.

To rebuild both climates from the recorded untouched sources and landmarks, run from `fun/transport`:

```sh
python3 tools/repack-farm-cores-registered.py
```

Use `--biome taiga` or `--biome desert` to rebuild one climate. For new generation, build the canonical job with `node tools/generate-building-prompt.mjs generation-job.json generation-prompt.json`, retain the exact final edit instructions and their source hashes, and measure the resulting architecture before registration.
