# Generated world sprites

Original artwork was created with the **built-in image_gen tool**, using the generated houses as the style reference. Transparent 256×256 per-object masters are downscaled into isolated 16, 32, 64 and 128-pixel cells. Current industry version 3 artwork uses the shared scale and style in `sprite-art-direction.js`, with an additional 256-pixel runtime level for five-tile compounds at larger display densities.

The collection includes 54 established civic/commercial building variants (18 identities in three climates, including town workshops), 48 additional park, mall and shop-exterior cutouts, and 33 placeholder town-feature cutouts (11 identities in three climates). Active industry families contain 47 supported industry/climate combinations, including food chains, plus ten fixed-size farm cores. There are also 72 nature objects, nine infrastructure pieces, nine cargo materials, and **72 individually drawn vehicle frames** (nine vehicle types × eight headings). Houses live in [../houses](../houses/README.md).

## Isometric camera

The game uses a fixed 2:1 dimetric view: ground axes slope ±26.565°, while walls, poles, trees and people stay upright. The architecture, industries, vehicles, ports, stops, tunnel mouths and rocky terrain have been redrawn for this view with the built-in imagegen tool. All three climate finishes retain the same camera. Ground textures and continuous roads are projected once; upright sprites are drawn separately in depth order. The same generated identities appear in the interface.

- Houses: all nine identities have three architectural designs and two authored orientations in each climate (162 sprites), with smaller structures and fenced gardens. Saved tile variants choose a stable design and orientation. Garden ground reveals the detailed world terrain. Sources and prompt records are in [../houses](../houses/README.md).
- Civic/commercial buildings: 54 sprites. The full civic and commerce collections were regenerated on 5 October 2026. Civic source sheets remain in `buildings-civic/{biome}`; active commerce sheets are in `buildings-commerce-camera-v2/{biome}`, including the dedicated town workshop in the ninth cell. [Civic regeneration](buildings-civic/regeneration-2026-10-05.json) and [commerce regeneration](buildings-commerce-camera-v2/regeneration-2026-10-05.json) retain exact prompts and source hashes. [Original architecture prompts](isometric-architecture-prompts.json) and [earlier commerce corrections](buildings-commerce-camera-v2/generation-selective-v2.json) remain as history.
- Parks, malls and shop exteriors: 48 generated cutouts in [town-variety-v1](town-variety-v1/README.md) add three distinct parks, three shopping centres and two additional exteriors for each of the five established shops, in all three climates. Saved shop seeds independently select identity and exterior; map and inspector portraits share the same design.
- Town features: parks, play and sport, the town hall and three newer shops are simple placeholder images rendered from their code drawings, waiting for painted art; [buildings-town-features](buildings-town-features/README.md) gives their cell order, camera and plate.
- Industries: 47 current version 3 sprites across the six `industries-*` and two [food-industry-v1](food-industry-v1/README.md) climate sheets. Five-tile compounds add halls, bays and equipment at the shared human scale, rather than enlarging doors and windows. Ten [farm-cores-v1](farm-cores-v1/README.md) sprites retain a 2×2 building yard inside each current 5×5 field plot. `source-scale-v3.png`, `generation-job.json`, exact `generation-prompt.json` and `provenance-scale-v3.json` identify the active sources; earlier isometric sources and prompts remain as history.
- Vehicles: all 72 directional frames now use `vehicle-{kind}-dimetric-v2/`; each directory records its source, prompt and packing provenance. Six families were regenerated against a mathematical camera guide; the other three retain their generated art with corrected shared-scale packing. Cargo is projected onto the vehicle's ground plane. The generic palette/fallback sheet in `vehicles-dimetric-v2/` uses these same southeast frames.
- Infrastructure: two stops, four separately drawn harbor orientations and eight road/rail tunnel mouths; [structure prompts](isometric-infrastructure-v2/isometric-prompt.json), [portal prompts](isometric-portals-v2/isometric-prompt.json), [structure atlas](isometric-infrastructure-v2/atlas.png), [portal atlas](isometric-portals-v2/atlas.png). Rear-facing entrances show the back of the masonry rather than a front-facing hole. Uniform runtime scaling preserves the authored camera in both map and UI portraits.
- City ground: nine regenerated overhead material cutouts in `city-ground-v3/`: asphalt, rail ballast, both bridge decks, road/rail junction materials, and lawn, paved and gravel zone plots. Connected road arms retain their width and terrain projection; zones retain their colored tint and dashed outline.
- Grass: deterministic ground-plane swards, fine blade clumps, earth grains and seed heads are baked by `grass-art.js` into the existing terrain meshes. World coordinates stabilize chunk edges and reduced Region detail keeps the distant landscape quiet.
- Terrain: 18 rock and mountain details; [prompts](isometric-terrain-prompts.json) and `source-isometric.png` in `nature-rocks/` and `nature-mountains/`.

Original RGBA pixels are registered into padded cells before downscaling. `prepare-building-atlas.py` handles irregular disconnected cutouts; `prepare-terrain-atlas.py` handles transparent gutters with configurable rows and columns. Both retain the generated alpha rather than repainting silhouettes. The normal atlas pipelines then make transparent 256px masters and isolated display densities.

The [camera audit](camera-audit.md) records the corrected families, runtime geometry, verification and remaining painterly variation. Generated artwork approximates the mathematical projection; the renderer's terrain and cargo transforms use it exactly.

## Source and prompt records

- `buildings-civic/` and `buildings-commerce/`: school, hospital, police, fire, stadium, church, pub, five shops and five services. Each folder records the initial prompt, climate edits and any layout repairs.
- `industries-{biome}/`, `industries-{biome}-extra/`, `food-industry-v1/{biome}/` and `farm-cores-v1/{biome}/`: current physically calibrated version 3 compounds or two-tile farm yards. Each records the canonical generation job, exact final generation/edit prompt, untouched source, uniform registration and provenance. `source-scale-v3-reference.png` retains a reference when a corrective edit needed it. Earlier `prompt.json`, isometric sources and isolated-silhouette records remain historical.
- `nature-*/`: tree species, bare trees, saplings, flowers, shrubs, rocks and mountains. Each folder contains its prompt records and packing provenance.
- [Terrain realism prompts](terrain-realism-prompts.json): the replacement rock and mountain sheets use lower, near-overhead outcrops, muted sediment and melting snow. Their `source-generated-v2.png` originals and `source-packed-v2.json` registration records are retained in the corresponding family folders. Packing preserves complete objects through transparent gutters, then produces the normal 256px masters and display densities.
- `vehicle-{kind}-dimetric-v2/`: active eight-direction sheets and their source/provenance. All use the verified compass order NW, N, NE / W, empty, E / SW, S, SE. Older `vehicle-{kind}/` artwork remains as source history.
- [transport-prompts.json](transport-prompts.json): original infrastructure and vehicle reference sheet prompts. The active vehicle fallback is assembled from the corrected directional masters.
- `cargo/`: textured coal, ore, logs, grain, crates, steel, barrels, glass and fish loads. The rendered amount follows the vehicle's capacity fraction; the body stays visible beneath it.

No direction is manufactured by rotating or mirroring another direction. Fronts, rears, windows, wheels and decks use the appropriate fixed-camera view. `vehicle-directions.js` quantizes travel angles into E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, N and NE. Train coaches select the direction of their own track segment. Night lights and cargo follow the same heading.

## Rebuilding

`tools/build-world-atlases.py` validates alpha and cell boundaries, normalizes cutouts, then uses premultiplied-alpha Lanczos downscaling and mild interior sharpening. The script never generates or repaints artwork. Use `--anchor center --vehicle --shared-scale` for directional sheets drawn at one physical scale: a common fit preserves their authored foreshortening, especially the shorter north/south views. Independent principal-axis normalization is retained only for rebuilding legacy sheets; it must not be used for the active camera-guide artwork. Atlas metadata includes the source SHA256, cell order, shared scale and source crop bounds.

Current calibrated industry and farm-core sheets use `tools/register-industry-atlas.py` first, then `--aligned --preserve-grid-scale --no-sharpen --max-cell 256`. One source-cell scale applies to the whole family, with clear filtering gutters and no independent bounding-box fitting or tiny-feature sharpening. This also preserves the camera when generated sheets have rectangular rather than square cell framing. Their family READMEs contain complete rebuild commands. New generation jobs use `tools/generate-building-prompt.mjs`, which derives their metre dimensions and visual style from the shared contract; exact corrective edit instructions remain in the final prompt record.

Some generated sheets extend into their empty center cell. Their local `prepare-source.py` scripts isolate complete objects through transparent gaps and integer-translate them into padded cells, without resampling or rotation. The replacement terrain sheets use `tools/prepare-terrain-atlas.py` for the same lossless registration through per-row transparent gutters. Run these before the shared packer.

The runtime fetches only the display atlases, never source sheets or masters. Each display resolution becomes usable as it loads, so a missing density can use another resolution of the same generated image. Native canvas drawing is the final fallback when no image is usable. Late loads invalidate artwork caches without changing the saved world; connectivity recovery retries missing densities. Window light anchors are measured against the generated building art and follow the climate actually drawn.

## Gameplay footprints

New industries reserve **5×5 tiles** for placement, inspection, selection and demolition. Farms keep their separate 2×2 building core, with terrain-rendered fields and perimeter fences occupying the rest of the five-tile plot. Roads, houses, trees and settlement growth cannot occupy a reserved site. Catchment, infrastructure access and operating-cost service benefits measure from every site edge. Current recipe 10 generates uniformly five-tile industries while preserving natural elevation beneath sites and settlements. Published recipes 1–9 reconstruct their original geography, including recipe 9's seven-tile farms. Compact saved industries expand only when adjoining land is free; blocked sites retain their saved extent to protect existing construction. Their native artwork keeps human-sized features at the common metre scale instead of shrinking a five-tile compound into a smaller world parcel.
