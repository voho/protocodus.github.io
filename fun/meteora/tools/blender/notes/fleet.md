# Fleet models: Mite, Talon, Warden, Halberd

Generated by `tools/blender/build_fleet.py` (shared helpers in
`tools/blender/fleet_parts.py`), which also writes
`js/anchors/{mite,talon,warden,halberd}.js`. Do not edit the GLBs or those
modules by hand: change the scripts and rebuild.

```
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
  --python fun/meteora/tools/blender/build_fleet.py [-- mite talon warden halberd]
```

The four models build in about 2½ minutes on an idle Apple-silicon Mac
(about 5 minutes with other Blender jobs competing for the CPU), mostly
Cycles bakes. `METEORA_FAST=1` gives a quick geometry pass (256² textures,
one sample, about 20 s per model); `METEORA_CHECK=1` also reports any part
that floats free of the hull, has open edges or faces inward. Previews, on
the CPU:

```
METEORA_PREVIEW_ENGINE=CYCLES /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background \
  --factory-startup --python fun/meteora/tools/blender/render_previews.py -- mite talon warden halberd
```

`tests/models-fleet-check.mjs` (run with `node tests/models-fleet-check.mjs`
from `fun/meteora`) checks the budgets, the WebP textures and their sizes,
the length along the nose axis, that every anchor in each module is a node of
its GLB at the position the module gives, the nozzle directions and sizes,
the turret node pairs, the colliders and the small craft's RCS coverage.

24,000 / 2.2 MB, Halberd 26,000 / 2.4 MB.

## The ships

All four follow the `build_ships.py` pipeline: bmesh parts (lofted
polygon hulls, lathed engines, airfoil and diamond blades, slabs, shells)
with procedural paint, joined into one `hull`, unwrapped and baked in
Cycles: normals from a bevelled copy, then base colour, roughness,
metallic and emission. The paint is `build_ships.weathered` made
size-aware (`fleet_parts.finish`): panel size, AO reach, edge-wear radius,
noise features and bump depth all scale with the ship, and the capital
ships carry a second, larger layer of plate seams, so detail shows at two
scales. Emission carries eyes, seams, windows, lights and engine throats
(the game multiplies it by 3).

### Mite (`mite.glb`): Harrow swarm drone

4.9 m from the mandible tips to the nozzle exit, 4.5 m span. A compact,
beetle-like faceted body: two oxblood wing cases over the back with a
glowing red seam between them, an oxblood brow over one big red eye that
fills the blunt face (hexagonal socket, iris and hot core), splayed
mandible spikes either side, a chin gun under the eye, two short
forward-swept blade wings drooping like legs (hazard bands, spiked tips),
a dorsal tine between the wing cases, one octagonal engine with an orange
throat. Gunmetal and oxblood. Head-on it reads as a red eye in a dark
mask; at range, an eye, an engine glow and a small dark blob: small and
many.

### Talon (`talon.glb`): Harrow heavy fighter

12.9 m long, 11.3 m span. Broader, heavier and better armed than the Thorn.
A wide faceted body under three stepped oxblood and gunmetal armour plates
(each prouder than the one ahead, with red light glowing in the gap at each
front edge) and cheek armour down the nose; a raised red eye in a hexagonal
socket ahead of the plates; two heavy gun barrels with cooling rings and
muzzle brakes along the nose sides; armoured stub wings ending in knuckles
from which two forward-swept claw tines curve ahead like talons; a missile
rack under each wing root with three warheads showing; two octagonal
nacelles with canted tail blades, radiator vents and orange throats; red
seams along the chines.

### Warden (`warden.glb`): Harrow escort corvette

140 m long, 37 m wide, 41 m from the tine tips to the tail blades. A long
faceted wedge rising to the stern. The blunt prow is a face: a great red
eye (3.2 m lens) under an oxblood brow, a plough-share ram below and two
pincer tines either side; four small red secondary eyes run down each
shoulder. Behind the eye, an oxblood "brain case" with sensor spines
(`sub_bridge`). Two keel beams carry twelve plough tines, splayed outward
and hooked forward at the tips. Armour plates step along the ridge
(alternating oxblood and dark gunmetal) and down the flanks, with groups of
glowing gill slits; a red seam glows along both chines; a short crest of
raked tines on the ridge, low enough for the ridge turrets to fire over;
twin raked blades on the aft shoulders with red beacons on their tips. An armoured engine block aft with radiator fins,
glowing exhaust vents and hazard-striped rims carries three nozzles (exit
radius 5.9 m). Four turrets: two on the ridge, two in sponsons on the lower
flanks.

### Halberd (`halberd.glb`): Belt Watch gunship

160 m long, 66 m across the nacelles, 46 m from keel to masthead. A stocky,
angular hull in Watch off-white over a graphite underside, with off-white
armour belts along the sides, an orange pinstripe, an orange bow band and
chevron, and the hull number 07 in graphite block digits on both bows
(reading left to right from either side). A knife-edge stem with an armoured
prow cap and a forward spike; a raised forecastle under the forward turrets;
a stepped superstructure amidships-aft (deckhouse, a bridge with a lit window
band and bridge wings, a mast with a sensor dome, yard and whips, a comms
dish, a radiator block) and a fire-control director ahead of it. Two decks
of warm lit windows (`#ffd9a0`) along each side and one row on the
chamfers. Six twin turrets: a superfiring pair on the forecastle, one aft on
the quarterdeck facing aft, three under the keel (two facing forward, one
aft). Two big nacelles on pylons at the stern quarters with intakes and
spinners, cooling fins, orange bands and blue-white throats (exit radius
7.1 m). RCS quads at the bow and stern quarters, point-defence blisters on
the chamfers. A hangar slot in the stern between the nacelles: a real
12 m-deep tunnel, lit inside, with a hazard-striped frame (`bay_0`).

## Anchors

Each `js/anchors/<name>.js` has the shape the models brief fixes (every
list present, empty when unused), in glTF space and metres: nose along −Z,
top +Y, right +X. Positions come from the empties in the GLB (all children
of `hull`, which sits at the origin unrotated), so a module's `pos` is also
the node's translation.

| | Mite | Talon | Warden | Halberd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `radius` / `length` | 2.87 / 4.89 | 7.67 / 12.88 | 71.59 / 140 | 85.75 / 160.1 |
| nozzles (`size` = exit radius) | `nozzle_C` 0.36 | `nozzle_L/R` 0.63 | `nozzle_0` (top), `_1` (port), `_2` (starboard) 5.9 | `nozzle_0` (port), `_1` (starboard) 7.1 |
| muzzles | `muzzle_C` (chin gun) | `muzzle_L/R` (gun barrels) | — | — |
| launchers | — | `launcher_0` (port), `_1` (starboard) rack mouths | — | — |
| RCS | 8 | 16 | — | — |
| cockpit (`cam_cockpit`) | at the eye | at the eye | — | — |
| colliders `col_NN` | — | — | 72 | 56 |
| subsystems | — | — | `sub_bridge` r 6, `sub_engine_0` r 15 | `sub_bridge` r 8, `sub_engine_0` (port) / `_1` (starboard) r 10 |
| turrets | — | — | `turret_00..03`, size 4.5 | `turret_00..05`, size 4.96 |
| bays | — | — | — | `bay_0`: hangar mouth, launching aft (+Z) |
| lights | — | — | `light_0..2`: red beacons, blink 2.4 s (blade tips) and 1.2 s (ram) | `light_0` red (port nacelle), `light_1` green (starboard), `light_2/3` white strobes (masthead, keel), blink 1.5 s, `light_4/5` steady white (stern, bow) |

- **Nozzles**: exhaust along the empty's Blender local −Y, glTF ≈ +Z;
  `size` is the exit radius.
- **RCS**: `dir` is the puff direction (the port pushes the ship along
  −`dir`). The Talon follows the Kestrel/Thorn layout (nose and tail up,
  down, left, right; knuckle tips up and down; two forward-facing nose
  ports; two aft-facing tail ports). The Mite's eight ports still give
  every linear direction and every turn at least two ports: the head pair
  puffs out and forward, the engine pair out and aft, and the wingtips
  (ahead of the centre) up and down, so they pitch and roll as well.
- **Turrets** are mesh nodes, not empties. `turret_NN` is the house: origin
  at the yaw pivot on its barbette, local up = the turret's `up`, local
  forward = `fwd` (the barrels' rest direction). Its child `turret_NN_gun`
  has its origin on the trunnion, 2.18 m (Warden) or 2.17 m (Halberd)
  above the pivot. In three.js, yaw a turret by rotating `turret_NN` about
  its local +Y and elevate the guns by rotating `turret_NN_gun` about its
  local +X (positive raises the barrels; at rest they point along the gun's
  local −Z). Each gun node carries an empty `turret_NN_muzzle` at the
  barrels' tip (centred between the Halberd's twin barrels; 10.5 m
  (Warden) or 14.6 m (Halberd) ahead of the trunnion), so its world
  position is the bolt's spawn point however the turret is aimed. `size` is
  the house's radius, a sensible hit radius for the turret subsystem. The
  Warden's turrets: `turret_00` ridge forward, `_01` ridge aft, `_02`
  starboard and `_03` port sponsons (their up tilts 43° outboard from
  straight down). The Halberd's: `_00` forecastle, `_01` superfiring behind
  it, `_02` quarterdeck facing aft, `_03..05` under the keel (`_05` facing
  aft). The Warden's ridge turrets fire clear over the crest fore and aft;
  its sponsons cover the flanks and the space below. The Halberd's keel
  turrets have the whole lower hemisphere; above the deck, the superfiring
  pair clears each other firing forward, but low fire aft from `_00`/`_01`
  and forward from `_02` crosses the superstructure, so the game may want
  to mask those bearings (as a real ship's fire control would).
- **Colliders**: `col_NN` empties, uniform scale = radius, drawn as spheres
  in Blender. They are fitted by `fleet_parts.fit_colliders`: the hull and
  turrets are voxel-remeshed into one watertight surface, grid points inside
  it (by ray parity) become candidate centres with the largest sphere that
  fits, grown by 1 m, and spheres are picked greedily to cover the most of
  the surface. No sphere stands more than about 1 m proud of the hull, and
  about 84 % of the surface lies within 1.6 m of a sphere; the rest is
  thin blades and antennae. The Warden's tines are too thin for the grid,
  so each carries two hand-placed spheres (24 of its 72).
- **Subsystems**: `sub_<kind>[_n]`, uniform scale = hit radius; `kind` is
  the second word of the name (`bridge`, `engine`). The Warden's bridge is
  the brain case behind the eye.
- `nodes` is `{}` for all four: the only animated nodes are the turrets,
  which `turrets` names.

## Known weaknesses

- `render_previews.py` frames the bounding sphere at 0.8, which trims the
  ends of the two long hulls a little in the three-quarter preview.
- The Warden's and Halberd's tail blades, pylons and antennae, and the tips
  of the Warden's tines and pincers, are thinner than any collider and
  partly uncovered; bolts that miss them hit the hull spheres behind.
- The Talon's claw tips reach 7.7 m from its origin, beyond the 6.5 m
  collision radius in `js/catalog.js`.
- Faces in full shadow (the inner faces of the Warden's tail blades, the
  Talon's fins) bake and render very dark, as the dark Harrow paint does
  everywhere under a single key light.
