How to play

One line, every cell.

  • 1Start on 1, finish on the last number. The line passes the numbers in order.
  • Fill the board. Every cell is visited exactly once, moving between neighbouring cells.
  • Walls stop the line. It cannot cross a glowing wall, or itself.

Drag from the head of the line, or tap neighbouring cells. Retrace the line to erase, or use Undo. On a keyboard, ← ↑ → ↓ draw and ⌫ steps back. U undo · N new puzzle · R reset · H hint · M sound · T theme. Try a Daily puzzle, or New for an endless supply. Six themes bring their own scenery, colours and weather. Every board has exactly one solution. Hints highlight a route or a wrong turn; you draw or retrace the line yourself.
