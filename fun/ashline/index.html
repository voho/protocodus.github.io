New to command? Field notes +

Every refinery includes a hauler that automatically collects and delivers minerals. Production buildings train in parallel. Select one and use Rally (R) to choose where new units go. Save or load your operation from the pause menu. Build a barracks for rifle teams and anti-armor rocket infantry, then a vehicle factory for armor. Rocket towers defend your base with explosive missiles. Armed units automatically fire at nearby enemies while guarding. Every 5 kills earns a rank, up to 3; each adds 20% base damage, speed, and maximum HP. Reactors keep production running. Lava pools block movement and construction; units find a route around them. Select units and use Explore (X) to automatically scout into the fog. Destroy the enemy core to win.

Mouse: left click or drag to select; double click a unit to select all of its type on screen; right click to move, attack, or harvest. Touch: tap a unit, then tap its destination; drag to pan. Pause at any time for the full field guide.

Every completed refinery deploys one free hauler. Select a barracks, factory, or refinery and right click a destination (or use Rally / R) to direct its new units. Each production building runs its own queue in parallel; one unit trains at a time per building. Select a compatible producer to recruit there, or let recruitment choose the least busy available producer. Card badges count queued units across your base; the separate bars show each active producer’s progress. Look inside a production bay to see its current unit taking shape. Idle bays are empty and quiet. Hauler hoppers visibly fill and empty, and delivered shards pass through the refinery before its machinery settles. Save and load one operation in this browser from this pause menu, or load it from the briefing next time. Haulers automatically collect finite minerals and deliver their loads, resuming after move or stop orders. Selections containing military units automatically leave haulers out; select haulers on their own to command them together. Armed units guard by default, firing at visible enemies in range without chasing them. Move to reposition, or attack move to engage along the way. Explore (X) sends selected units, including haulers, into reachable unexplored areas. Armed explorers pause to fire at nearby visible enemies, then continue scouting. Toggle Explore again, stop, or issue a manual order to take control. Once exploration is complete, armed units guard and haulers resume harvesting. Lava pools cannot be crossed or built on; use the routes around their basalt banks. Construct near your existing base and keep power supply above demand; low power slows production and disables defensive towers. Units earn ranks at 5, 10, and 15 kills, gaining +20%, +40%, and +60% base damage, movement speed, and maximum HP. Three chevrons show rank; select one unit for its kills and next threshold. Rifle squads hold ground, rocket infantry counter armor, recon rovers counter infantry, tanks break defensive lines, and artillery outranges towers. Rocket towers require a barracks and reactor and launch missiles with a wider blast radius. Keep infantry away from clustered armor under rocket fire. Protect your core: losing it ends the operation.

The Red Foundry builds and harvests with the same economy. It scouts, defends, raids enemy infrastructure, and assembles attacks from what it discovers.
