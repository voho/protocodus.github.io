New to command? Field notes +

Factions & terrain: Organics fields humans, aliens, and rugged industrial armor. AI Unity commands precise robot cohorts and walkers. Each faction has its own buildings and units. Mountains, boulders, and twisted trees obstruct movement and construction; passable craters reduce direct-fire damage by 15% but cannot support buildings. Rockets and siege blasts ignore crater cover. Choose Wall, then drag a continuous line to preview its cost and build; leave openings for your own forces.

Research & upgrades: build a Signal laboratory to unlock three research branches. Upgrades strengthen existing and future forces. Select a structure, then Upgrade, to increase its operating speed or power efficiency. A foundry with an Advanced assembly bay and Advanced ballistics can produce Pike strikers. Field engineers automatically repair nearby machinery for credits. Selected buildings also have Repair and Sell buttons with immediate costs and refunds.

Expansion: each completed nexus supports 200 units, up to 2,000. Recruit a Nexus construction vehicle (or Mainframe constructor) at a vehicle factory, move it to a scouted mineral field, select Deploy nexus, and choose clear ground within 4 tiles. The vehicle becomes a new nexus; after construction, build refineries and defenses around it. Losing a nexus reduces capacity but keeps existing units alive. Units steer while moving and keep space between their bodies. A numbered badge beside each assigned unit shows its exclusive control group.

Grid & minerals: spare power provides a modest construction and production bonus with diminishing returns, up to 25%. Capacitors charge from spare power and keep the base online during outages. When their reserves empty, defenses shut down and construction, production, research, unloading and repairs slow with available electricity. Protect your reactors. Mint and blue shards provide standard yield; red deposits are twice as dense, with twice the reserve and extraction rate. All colors convert into credits. Selected units show their individual assigned destinations; each vehicle settles into its own slot.

Every refinery includes a hauler that automatically collects and delivers minerals. Production buildings train in parallel. Select one and use Rally (R) to choose where new units go. Save or load your operation from the pause menu. Build a barracks for rifle teams and anti-armor rocket infantry, then a vehicle factory for armor. Rocket towers defend your base with explosive missiles. Guard units hold position and fire at the closest visible enemy in range. Defend units retaliate against attackers, pursuing only within their original firing radius. Choose an idle stance in the selection panel; commands temporarily use Guard, then restore your preference. Selected combat units show their firing ranges. Every 5 kills earns a rank, up to 3; each adds 20% base damage and maximum HP and 5% speed. Walls do not count toward ranks. Reactors keep production running. Lava pools block movement and construction; units find a route around them. Select units and use Explore (X) to automatically scout into the fog. Destroy all enemy nexuses and construction vehicles to win.

Mouse: left click or drag to select; double click a unit to select all of its type on screen; right click to gather, attack, or harvest; hold right click and drag to preserve and rotate the formation before releasing. Touch: tap a unit, then tap its destination; drag to pan; pinch to zoom. Pause at any time for the full field guide.

Every completed refinery deploys one free hauler. Select a barracks, factory, or refinery and right click a destination (or use Rally / R) to direct its new units. Each production building runs its own queue in parallel; one unit trains at a time per building. Select a compatible producer to recruit there, or let recruitment choose the least busy available producer. Card badges count queued units across your base; the separate bars show each active producer’s progress. Look inside a production bay to see its current unit taking shape. Idle bays are empty and quiet. Hauler hoppers visibly fill and empty, and delivered shards pass through the refinery before its machinery settles. Save and load one operation in this browser from this pause menu, or load it from the briefing next time. Haulers automatically collect finite minerals and deliver their loads, resuming after move or stop orders. Selections containing military units automatically leave haulers out; select haulers on their own to command them together. Armed units use Guard by default: hold position and fire at the closest visible enemy in range. Defend retaliates only against attackers and pursues them within the original firing radius. Set either idle stance in the selection panel. Commands temporarily use Guard; Defend resumes when the command ends. Faint circles show each selected combat unit’s current firing range. Move to reposition, or attack move to engage along the way. Explore (X) sends selected units, including haulers, into reachable unexplored areas. Armed explorers pause to fire at nearby visible enemies, then continue scouting. Toggle Explore again, stop, or issue a manual order to take control. Once exploration is complete, armed units guard and haulers resume harvesting. Lava pools cannot be crossed or built on; use the routes around their basalt banks. Construct near your existing base and keep power supply above demand; low power slows production and disables defensive towers. Units earn ranks at 5, 10, and 15 kills, gaining +20%, +40%, and +60% base damage and maximum HP and +5%, +10%, and +15% movement speed. Destroyed walls count for your side but not toward ranks. Three chevrons show rank; select one unit for its kills and next threshold. Rifle squads hold ground, rocket infantry counter armor, recon rovers counter infantry, tanks break defensive lines, and artillery outranges towers. Rocket towers require a barracks and reactor and launch missiles with a wider blast radius. Keep infantry away from clustered armor under rocket fire. Protect your command network: losing every nexus and construction vehicle ends the operation.

Command feedback: messages stack briefly at the top left of the battlefield; click the ⌖ beside one, or press Backspace, to center on it, and the tactical map pings recent alerts. The Idle and Bays buttons count units and buildings waiting for orders. Hover a console card for its damage per second, range, armor and counters, or a visible unit for its callsign, health and rank. Your units answer on the comms line above the tactical map (top left on phones), and veterans are named when they are promoted or lost; a promotion won out of sight is reported when you next select the unit. Edge scrolling, screen shake and tooltips can be switched off in this menu.

The opposing faction researches, upgrades its production, and builds and harvests with the same economy. It scouts mineral fields, deploys new nexuses, builds remote refineries, defends its bases, and raids from what it discovers.
