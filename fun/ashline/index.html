Every completed refinery deploys one free hauler. Haulers automatically collect finite minerals and deliver their loads, resuming after move or stop orders. Armed units guard by default, firing at visible enemies in range without chasing them. Move to reposition, or attack move to engage along the way. Explore (X) sends selected units, including haulers, into reachable unexplored areas. Armed explorers pause to fire at nearby visible enemies, then continue scouting. Toggle Explore again, stop, or issue a manual order to take control. Once exploration is complete, armed units guard and haulers resume harvesting. Construct near your existing base and keep power supply above demand; low power slows production and disables turrets. Rifle squads hold ground, recon rovers counter infantry, tanks break defensive lines, and artillery outranges turrets. Protect your core: losing it ends the operation.

The Red Foundry builds and harvests with the same economy. It scouts, defends, raids exposed harvesters, and assembles attacks from what it discovers.
