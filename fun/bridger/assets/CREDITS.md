# Bridger art

All images here were generated for Bridger on OpenArt in November 2026 with
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst as hand-painted, high-resolution art, then cropped,
resized and saved as WebP by script (vehicles keyed off a flat magenta
background with soft alpha).

| Files | What | Prompt gist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sky-valley.webp`, `sky-mesa.webp`, `sky-snow.webp`, `sky-night.webp`, `sky-forest.webp`, `sky-sandstone.webp`, `sky-dunes.webp`, `sky-city.webp` | 3840 px wide backdrops, cropped at the horizon | Hazy painted panorama in cool muted blues and violets, no green, yellow, orange or red |
| `rock-*.webp` | 512 px tiling rock for the banks | Seamless painted rock or earth texture in the same palette |
| `car.webp`, `truck.webp`, `loco.webp`, `wagon.webp` | 900 px vehicle sprites | Painted side view on flat magenta |

The wheel positions each sprite is drawn by live in `js/render.js` (`SPRITES`).
