# Bridger art

Bridger's panoramas, terrain textures and vehicle art were generated for the game
using Codex image generation in November 2026, then prepared as WebP assets.

## Painted worlds

The thirteen 2172 × 724 panoramas share a gouache treatment and a quiet central
area behind the bridge. The subject of each landscape prompt is listed below.

| File | Prompt subject |
| --- | --- |
| `sky-valley.webp` | Summer alpine valley with sunlit limestone and open pasture |
| `sky-mesa.webp` | Sienna mesas fading into violet twilight |
| `sky-snow.webp` | Blue glacier, snowy alpine peaks and an open winter sky |
| `sky-night.webp` | Moonlit alpine landscape in petrol blue and silver |
| `sky-forest.webp` | Misty green woodland with layered broadleaf and spruce canopies |
| `sky-sandstone.webp` | Daylit canyon with weathered red sandstone |
| `sky-dunes.webp` | Moonlit desert with mauve and silver dune crests |
| `sky-city.webp` | City lights and a waterfront beneath a deep blue evening sky |
| `sky-desert.webp` | Daylit Sonoran desert, pale turquoise sky, sandstone mesas, dunes and saguaros |
| `sky-meadow.webp` | Summer sky over rolling wildflower fields dotted with daisies, buttercups and trees |
| `sky-island.webp` | Tropical island coast with palms and a lagoon |
| `sky-jungle.webp` | Layers of tropical jungle canopy |
| `sky-lake.webp` | Canadian conifer forest surrounding a lake |

## Terrain and vehicles

The eight 768 × 768 `rock-*.webp` tiles depict alpine schist, sandstone at
twilight, frosted granite, moonlit slate, woodland loam, terracotta sediment,
compacted desert sand and urban embankment aggregate. Each prompt specified
fine, evenly distributed brushwork and tileable edges. The renderer colours
the painted texture for each world and shades it with depth.

The 1024 px `car.webp`, `truck.webp`, `loco.webp` and `wagon.webp` are
side-on, wheel-less bodies with transparent backgrounds. The independent
256 px `wheel-road.webp` and `wheel-rail.webp` paintings turn at each axle.

The game card shows a frame from a simulated truck crossing in the Sandstone
world on the river with seed `sunset`.
