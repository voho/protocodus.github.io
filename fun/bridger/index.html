How to play

Build a bridge, then press Go!

  • Start at a red boltDrag from a bolt or joint to draw a beam. Gold dots on existing beams show where you can attach another one.
  • The road is already thereBrace it so the vehicle can cross. Double-click or double-tap a piece to erase it. Drag along the deck to replace missing road.
  • Triangles are strongWood is cheap, steel is strong and long. Cables are light and only pull, like a rope.
  • Watch the coloursGreen beams are relaxed, red ones are close to snapping. Make the red ones stronger.

Bridge ideas

For grown-upsGrid points are 2 m apart. Tap a support with another material to swap it. Double-click, double-tap or use Erase on any bridge piece to remove it. Erasing a joint removes every piece attached there. Undo restores the last edit. Start or end a drag at a gold dot to connect to the middle of wood, steel or cable. Uprights and diagonal braces sit beside the lane. Leave room above the vehicle for horizontal beams. Joints must stay 1 m above the water and out of the rock. The road comes laid in 2 m pieces and counts toward the cost. Each material has a longest beam: wood 6 m, steel 8 m, cable 20 m. A member breaks when its force passes its strength; the colour shows that force as a share of the strength, and the last test's peak stays on the beams while you build. The physics is a position-based (XPBD) truss with self-weight, so bridges sag, sway and fall for real. Every river is generated from its seed and tested with a design the game built and drove across; Easy gives 50% more money than that design costs, Medium 25% and Hard 10%. Solution builds a fresh random design that has passed the same test within your budget. Space test · 1–4 tools · U undo · N new river · M sound.
